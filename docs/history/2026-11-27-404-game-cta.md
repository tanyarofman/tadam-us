# 404 page as a game round with a buy CTA, November 27, 2026

Tanya asked to make the 404 page more engaging, with the main goal of getting
visitors to buy and create a game.

The page is now a small round of the game: "Question 404: Where did this page
go?" with four answer pills and a 20-second countdown bar. Any answer, or time
running out, reveals D ("It's busy becoming your game show") in the game's
correct-answer green; a right pick shows "+1,000". The offer box then lights up
in yellow, and on phones the page scrolls to it.

The offer box is visible from the start (beside the question on desktop, under
it on phones, above the fold on a 375x812 screen): headline, the hero subline,
"Create my game" into /create (data-track cta_create_click, source 404), then
price from pricing.ts, the 14-day full refund and 24-hour delivery, the three
real customer photos with the average of their real ratings, and smaller links
to the live demo, home page and blog.

References: cta.gallery's CTA tips (risk-removing microcopy, social proof next
to the button, first-person button text, headline above the CTA, CTA above the
fold). styles.refero.design and cta.gallery have no 404 category.

Reduced motion: no countdown, no auto-reveal, no pulse or scroll. Without
JavaScript the answers do nothing and the CTA still works. The page stays noindex.
