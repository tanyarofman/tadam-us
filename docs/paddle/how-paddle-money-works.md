# How the money works with Paddle

## A sale

1. A buyer pays **$99 (Basic) or $149 (VIP)** on tadamevents.com. The charge is made by **Paddle** (the merchant of record), not by Tanya's business.
2. Paddle adds sales tax or VAT where required (that part goes to the tax authorities, not to us), and deducts its fee: about **5% + $0.50** per sale. Check the current rate in your Paddle account.
3. The rest goes to Tanya's **Paddle balance**. On a $99 sale that's roughly $93.55.

## Payouts

- Paddle pays out the balance on a schedule (usually monthly, once the balance passes the minimum), to the payout account set in Paddle.
- **Payout account:** a USD account at Tanya's Israeli bank, or Payoneer receiving details if Paddle accepts them. Payoneer avoids converting at bank rates. Check in Payoneer which details it gives you.

## Refunds

- The buyer asks for a refund (through Paddle, or by emailing us), and Paddle sends the money back to the buyer's card.
- **The refunded amount is deducted from Tanya's Paddle balance** ([Paddle: issuing refunds](https://www.paddle.com/help/manage/your-customers/how-do-i-issue-refunds)). Tanya never has to send money back herself.
- With a 14-day refund window, most refunds land **before** the monthly payout, so they simply reduce it. If a refund comes after a payout, it's taken from the next one.
- **To confirm with Paddle:** whether Paddle keeps its fee on a refunded sale ([retained fees](https://developer.paddle.com/changelog/2025/retained-fees-payout-totals/)) and how it handles a negative balance (more refunds than sales in a period).

## Chargebacks

A chargeback is when a buyer disputes the charge with their bank instead of asking for a refund.

- Paddle handles the dispute. It deducts the disputed amount **plus a non-refundable chargeback fee** from the balance.
- If Paddle wins, the amount comes back; the fee doesn't ([Paddle: chargebacks](https://www.paddle.com/help/manage/risk-prevention/understanding-chargebacks-with-paddle)).
- An easy 14-day refund keeps chargebacks low: people who can get a refund rarely go to their bank.

## Accounting (Morning)

- Tanya's business sells to **Paddle**, not to the end buyers. So there's **one invoice per payout**, issued to Paddle (Paddle.com Market Ltd, UK), in USD, for the net payout amount.
- For a עוסק מורשה this is normally an **export of services at 0% VAT**. The buyers' VAT and sales tax are Paddle's responsibility.
- Paddle provides a monthly statement for each payout. Keep it with the invoice.
- **Confirm with the accountant before the first payout:** the invoice details, the 0% VAT treatment, and how to record USD income.
