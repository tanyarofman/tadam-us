## Product description (about 100 words)

> Tadam! is a web app for creating a personalized trivia game show for a party, such as a birthday, bar mitzvah or wedding. The customer types in stories and adds photos about the guest of honor, picks one of our ready-made host characters and voices, and reviews AI-drafted questions in the editor. After payment, the finished game is delivered by email as a private link. At the party it runs in the browser: the host appears on a TV or projector and guests answer on their phones by scanning a QR code. No downloads, no subscription.

## How is the product delivered?

> Digitally. Within 24 hours of payment, the customer receives an email with a private link to their game. The link works for 30 days.

## Do you use AI?

> Yes, as part of the software. AI drafts trivia questions from the customer's own stories, and our ready-made host characters use synthetic stock voices and pre-made video. We do not generate likenesses or voices of real people: all hosts are invented characters, and our terms forbid impersonation. Customers review and edit every question before paying. We have opted out of AI training with our providers where that option exists, and customer content is deleted after 30 days.

## Don't say

- "We build the game for you" / "done for you" / "consulting" / "service": this reads as a human service.
- Anything about a custom host based on a real person.
- Prices or promises that don't match the site.
