## 1. Who we are

Tadam! is operated by Tanya Rofman, a sole trader. We are the "controller" of the personal data described here. Contact us about privacy at hey@tadamevents.com.

## 2. What we collect

| What | Examples | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Your account and order details | Name, email address, country | You, when you create a game and pay |
| Payment details | Card or PayPal details, billing address | Collected and handled by **Paddle**, our merchant of record. We never see your full card number |
| Game content | Stories, names, nicknames, details, photos and voice recordings about the guest of honor and other people | You, in the game creator |
| Guest play data | The nickname a guest types to join, their answers and score | Guests, when they play. Guests don't need an account or email |
| Technical data | IP address, browser type, pages visited, error logs | Automatically, when you use the site |
| Usage data (Google Analytics) | Pages viewed, buttons clicked, approximate location (city or country), device and browser type, how you found the site | Automatically, through Google Analytics, subject to your cookie choice (see Cookies) |

Game content often includes information about **other people, including children**, such as the guest of honor at a bar mitzvah or birthday. You may only upload it if you have their permission, or their parent's or guardian's permission for children (see our [Terms](/terms)). Please don't upload sensitive information such as health details, financial details or ID numbers.

## 3. Why we use it, and our legal basis (GDPR)

| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Creating, delivering and supporting your game | Performance of our contract with you |
| Processing payments, taxes and refunds (through Paddle) | Contract and legal obligation |
| Keeping the Service secure and fixing errors | Legitimate interests |
| Measuring how visitors use the site, to improve it (Google Analytics) | Consent in the EU, the UK and Switzerland; elsewhere, our legitimate interests |
| Sending service emails (your game link, receipts) | Contract |
| Marketing emails, only if you opt in | Consent, which you can withdraw at any time |

We don't sell your personal data, don't share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and don't use your game content to train AI models ourselves. We have opted out of AI training with every provider that offers it, and we choose providers that don't train on customer data.

We and some of our providers process data outside the EU and UK, including in the US. Where data leaves the EU or UK, we rely on adequacy decisions, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 6. How long we keep it

- **Game content and the game itself:** 30 days from delivery, then deleted. You can ask us to delete it sooner.
- **Order records:** as long as tax and accounting law requires (generally up to 7 years). These records don't include your game content.
- **Technical logs:** up to [[LOG RETENTION, e.g. 30 days]].

## 7. Children

The Service is for adults (18+) organizing events. We don't knowingly let children create games or give us their personal data directly. Children may play as guests by typing a nickname, without giving an email or creating an account. Information about children that a parent includes in a game is used only to make that game and is deleted after 30 days. If you believe a child has given us personal data directly, write to hey@tadamevents.com and we'll delete it.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to:

- access the data we hold about you, or get a copy of it
- correct it
- delete it
- restrict or object to how we use it
- withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent

**EU and UK residents** can also complain to their local data protection authority.

**California and other US state residents** have the right to know, delete and correct their data, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data. We don't sell or share personal data in that sense. We won't treat you differently for using your rights.

To use any right, write to hey@tadamevents.com. We reply within 30 days.

## 9. Cookies

We use the cookies and similar storage the site needs to work (for example, to keep your game draft while you create it), and Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. We don't use advertising cookies, and we have turned off Google's advertising features and Google signals.

| Cookie | Set by | What for | Kept for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_ga`, `_ga_*` | Google Analytics | Telling visits apart and counting them, so we see which pages and buttons are used | Up to 2 years |

**In the EU, the UK and Switzerland**, Google Analytics sets no cookies unless you click "Accept" on the cookie banner. Until then, and if you decline, Google receives only cookieless signals (for example, that a page was viewed) without an identifier for your device. **Elsewhere**, analytics cookies are on by default and you can turn them off at any time.

To change your choice, use "Cookie settings" (in the footer of every page). Google Analytics data is kept for 14 months. You can also block cookies in your browser or install Google's [Google Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## 10. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls and reputable providers. Game links are private and hard to guess, but anyone with the link can open the game, so share it only with your guests.

## 11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant, we'll tell you by email or on the site.
