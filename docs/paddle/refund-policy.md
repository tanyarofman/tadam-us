We want you to love your game. If you're not happy, you can get a **full refund within 14 days of purchase**, for any reason.

## How to ask for a refund

- Reply to your order confirmation email from Paddle, or
- Go to [paddle.net](https://paddle.net) and look up your order, or
- Write to us at hey@tadamevents.com with the email address you used to pay.

## What happens next

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com, the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Paddle processes the refund to your original payment method. Refunds usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

When a refund is issued, your game link stops working.

## Your legal rights

This policy is in addition to any rights you have under the law of your country, including the right of withdrawal for consumers in the EU and UK.
