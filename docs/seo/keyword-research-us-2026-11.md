# Keyword research for the US and Europe, 2026-11-24

First research round for the English site. DataForSEO, US (`location_code 2840`) as the main market, with the UK (2826), Ireland (2372) and Germany (2276, searches in English) for comparison.

**Spent:** $2.37 of the approved $10 (balance $42.98 before, $40.61 after).

**Data** (all in `docs/seo/data/`):
- `seeds-us-*.txt`: 251 seed keywords in six groups.
- `volume-us-2026-11-24.csv`: US volumes with peak month and the last 12 months.
- `suggestions-us-2026-11-24.csv`: 5,662 related keywords for 26 seeds.
- `serp-us-2026-11-24.csv`: top 10, AI Overview sources and "People also ask" for 50 keywords.
- `questions-us-2026-11-24.csv`: 857 questions and related searches for 18 topics.
- `volume-uk-*`, `volume-ie-*`, `volume-de-*`: the European shortlist (49 keywords).

## Findings

### 1. Nobody searches for the product. People search for the event.
Searches a month in the US:

People don't know a product like Tadam exists. What they search for is *what to do at the event*, and, very often, *questions about the people in the room*. The site has to describe Tadam in those words, and the blog brings the traffic.

### 4. Who ranks today: weak, scattered results
- Across 50 SERPs, the top sites are Pinterest (34 results), Reddit (8), YouTube (7), Facebook (7) and Instagram (6), followed by small blogs and a few app sites (pickmeupgame.com 7, crowdparty.app, geekpowered.games, triviamaker.com).
- The big wedding sites appear only sometimes: The Knot, Brides, WeddingWire, Paperless Post.
- Keyword difficulty is 0 to 10 for almost every event keyword. A well-structured article with real answers can rank.
- AI Overviews appear on 46 of the 50 SERPs. The sources they cite are The Knot, Reddit, Paperless Post, WeddingWire, The Bump, Pampers, Asana, Slack, SessionLab and Bryant University. This is the kind of source to link in our articles.
- Direct competitors (custom trivia or game show for events):
  - Apps people run themselves: crowdparty.app, pickmeupgame.com, triviamaker.com, Kahoot, Jackbox.
  - Live hosts: gameshowsalive.com, trivia hosts on Yelp.
  - None of them combines a game built from the guests' stories with a host on screen. That's Tadam's honest angle.

### 5. Questions people ask (they become the H2s)
- **Shoe game:**
  - How many questions should you ask in the shoe game?
  - How long does the shoe game take?
  - How do you introduce it, and how do you end it?
  - What are 20 questions about the bride?
- **Bridal shower:**
  - How many games are normal for a bridal shower?
  - What are fun games for a small group?
  - How do you make a bridal shower actually fun?
  - What can you do instead of games?
- **Rehearsal dinner:** What do you usually do at a rehearsal dinner? What fun activities are there besides eating?
- **40th and 50th birthdays:**
  - How do you throw a great 40th?
  - What games can you play with 40 people?
  - What do people usually do for their 50th?
  - What's a good budget for a 50th birthday? (We don't answer with prices.)
- **Retirement party:**
  - What happens at a retirement party?
  - How do you make a retirement party fun?
  - How long should it be?
  - What should I say?
- **Office party:** What are fun games for an office party? How do you play Secret Santa or White Elephant?
- **Team trivia:** How do you run trivia for teams? How do you host team trivia? Can you do trivia on Microsoft Teams?
- **How well do you know me:** variants for adults, the best friend, the birthday party, couples, and examples.
- **Family reunion:** What games do you play at a family reunion?

  | 40th birthday ideas | 6,600 | |
  | stag do ideas | 2,900 | |
  | pass the parcel | 2,400 | |

  "Quiz" is the UK word where the US says "trivia". American terms such as bridal shower, rehearsal dinner and shoe game are small in the UK.
- **Ireland:** about one tenth of the UK, with the same words.
- **Germany** (searches in English): very small, under 1,000 even for "general knowledge quiz". English-speaking continental Europe isn't worth targeting on its own; it's served by the US and UK articles.
- **Recommendation:** write in American English for the US. Add a small set of UK articles later: mr and mrs quiz, hen party games, christmas quiz, and pass the parcel (already written). Use UK wording in titles and the same product.

## Rules for the writer
- **Trademarks:** "Super Bowl" and "The Newlywed Game" are trademarks. Use them only to name the occasion or the kind of game people search for ("super bowl party games", "newlywed game questions"). No logos, no team or player names, no TV show content, and no claim or hint of a link to the NFL or the show.
- No real celebrities or characters. Example scenarios are clearly labeled.
- Sources: prefer The Knot, Brides and WeddingWire for wedding customs; SHRM, universities and the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics for workplace facts; the CDC or NIH only where health matters. Link only what we verified.
