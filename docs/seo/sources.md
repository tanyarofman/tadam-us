## Not answered by any source we found
- US team building article: whether an employer may require attendance is left to HR or an employment lawyer; the article cites only the federal rule on paid time (29 CFR 785.27), and notes that state rules can add to it.
- Whether pass the parcel may be played on Shabbat: no ruling about the game itself was found (only general rulings on tearing wrappers). The article says so and suggests asking a rabbi.
- When a bachelorette party is "usually" held: no authoritative source, so the article gives it as advice (two weeks to a month before the wedding), not as a fact.
- The 100 trivia answers in general-knowledge-trivia-questions (rewritten for the US on 2026-11-24) are standard reference facts; only the pub quiz history and NASA carry a source link. Re-check the list once a year (for example the number of planets or Knesset seats will not change, but "largest" and "fastest" records can).
- The 30 general trivia answers in team-trivia-for-work (2026-11-24) are standard reference facts. The less obvious ones were checked by web search: Q is the only letter in no US state name (several quiz sources agree), a group of flamingos is a flamboyance (Smithsonian's National Zoo, HowStuffWorks), koala fingerprints are almost identical to human ones (PBS NOVA, Live Science).
