## FAQ

**How does it work?**
Tell us about the guest of honor, pick a host, review the questions and pay. Your game arrives by email as a private link. At the party, open the link on the big screen and guests join by scanning the code.

**Can we see an example first?**
Of course. Tom's game shows you the host, the questions and the guests' experience. Open it on a computer and join from your phone with the code on the screen.

**How long does the game take?**
About 10 to 15 minutes. Long enough to get everyone excited, and short enough for the party to keep its pace.

**What do we need at the party?**
A TV or projector, an internet connection and a few minutes of everyone's attention. Guests scan the code on the screen and play in their phone's browser.

**How soon do we get the game?**
Within [[DELIVERY TIME]] of payment. Create it at least two days before the party to be safe.

**How long can we play it?**
Your private link works for 30 days from delivery. After that, the game and everything you uploaded are deleted.

**Who sees our photos and stories?**
Only you and the guests you share the game link with. We use your content only to make your game. We never sell it, never use it for advertising, and delete it after 30 days. See our [privacy policy](/privacy).

**Can we get a refund?**
Yes. You can ask for a full refund within 14 days of purchase. See our [refund policy](/refunds).

**Can the host look or sound like someone we know?**
No. All our hosts are invented characters. We don't create hosts that copy a real person's face or voice.

**Which parties is it right for?**
Birthdays, bar and bat mitzvahs, weddings, anniversaries, graduations, retirements, office parties and family reunions. The questions are built around your guest of honor and your crowd.
