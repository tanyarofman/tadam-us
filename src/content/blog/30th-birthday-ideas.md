Thirty is the first birthday that feels like a milestone and still feels like a party. Some people want a house full of friends, others want a quiet dinner and no fuss. Here are 30 ideas for both, from a dirty 30 party to a weekend away, and how to put their story at the center of the night.

## What is a good 30th birthday idea?

A good 30th birthday idea matches the person: a big party for the social one, a dinner or a trip for the lowkey one. Choose the format first, then add one theme and one moment that's about them, like a toast or a game.

1. **Ask what they want.** Some people dream of a big party, others would rather have six friends and a long dinner. If it's a surprise, ask their partner or best friend.
2. **Invite across their life.** College friends, work friends, family and old friends from home. At 30, many of them have never met.
3. **Choose the format.** At home, out or away. The table below sorts all 30 ideas.
4. **Send invitations early.** Paperless Post [recommends about four weeks ahead for a casual birthday and six for a formal one](https://www.paperlesspost.com/blog/when-to-send-out-birthday-party-invites).
5. **Plan one personal moment.** A toast, a slideshow of their twenties or a game about them. It's what guests remember.

## What are you supposed to do on your 30th birthday?

There's no rule: you're supposed to do whatever feels like you, whether that's a big party, a trip or a quiet dinner. Many people use 30 to look back on their twenties and celebrate with the friends from every part of them.

Thirty often lands in a busy year. The median age at first marriage in the US is about 31 for men and 29 for women, according to [Bowling Green State University's analysis of Census data](https://www.bgsu.edu/ncfmr/resources/data/family-profiles/fp-26-01.html), so a 30th often comes between friends' weddings and showers. That's one more reason to make it about the birthday person, and only them.

A few ways people mark the day:

- **A "30 before 30" list.** Thirty small things to do before the birthday, finished with friends on the day itself (idea 25).
- **Thirty notes.** Ask thirty friends and family members for one memory each, and give them in a box or a book (idea 26).
- **A day of their favorite things.** Their favorite breakfast, their favorite walk, their favorite restaurant and their favorite people at the end (idea 30).

## How do you celebrate a 30th birthday at home?

At home, choose a format that fits your space: a house party for a crowd, a dinner party for a dozen or a game night in the living room. Add one theme and one personal moment, and it feels planned without a venue.

- **House party (1).** Clear the furniture, make a playlist of the songs from their twenties and set up one drink station.
- **Dinner party (2).** A long table, their favorite dishes and one toast per course.
- **Game night (3).** Board games, card games, or a trivia round about the birthday person.
- **Trivia night about them (4).** Questions about their life, answered by the guests. Start from our [100 how well do you know me questions](/blog/how-well-do-you-know-me-questions/) and add a general round from our [100 trivia questions for adults](/blog/general-knowledge-trivia-questions/).
- **Backyard movie night (21).** A projector, blankets and a movie they loved as a teenager.
- **Potluck of favorite dishes (23).** Every guest brings a dish that reminds them of the birthday person, with a card explaining why.

## What are 30th birthday ideas for her and for him?

Plan around the person, not around a gender. A food lover gets a cooking class or a dinner party, someone who loves the outdoors gets a cabin weekend, and a social person gets a house party with everyone they know.

Some starting points by personality:

- **Food and drink:** a dinner party, a brewery tour or a cooking class (ideas 2, 17 and 16).
- **The outdoors:** a hike and picnic, a cabin weekend or a fire pit night (19, 12 and 22).
- **Being with everyone they know:** a house party, a dirty 30 party or a surprise party (1, 6 and 29).
- **A small circle:** a weekend away, a spa day or a city trip (11, 18 and 13).
- **Music:** karaoke, a concert or a 1997 throwback party (8, 20 and 7).
- **Games:** game night, an escape room or trivia about themselves (3, 14 and 4).

## What can you do for a 30th birthday cheaply?

Celebrate at home or outdoors, and spend on one thing that matters to the birthday person instead of on decorations. A potluck, a hike and picnic or a game night costs little and still feels special.

- **Potluck (23).** The guests bring the food, you bring the cake.
- **Hike and picnic (19).** A scenic trail, sandwiches and a bottle of something to toast with at the top.
- **Fire pit and s'mores (22).** A backyard, a fire and a few friends who stay late.
- **Thirty notes (26).** Costs nothing but a box, and it's often the gift they keep longest.
- **Game night (3).** Ask guests to bring their favorite game and a snack.

## Why do they call it the dirty 30?

"Dirty 30" is party slang that rhymes, not an old tradition, and it usually means a 30th with a wild night out. Today it's mostly a theme: black and gold decor, a "dirty 30" cake and a night of dancing.

Other themes that work for a 30th:

- **The year they were born.** Someone turning 30 in 2027 was born in 1997. Play the pop and hip-hop of the late 1990s, serve the snacks they ate as kids and ask guests to dress in the decade (idea 7).
- **Goodbye twenties.** A playful "funeral" for their twenties, with a eulogy from their best friend.
- **The Saturn return.** Astrologers say Saturn [returns to where it was at your birth around age 29 or 30](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturn_return), and the phrase has become a joke about the chaos of the late twenties. It makes a fun space theme.
- **Their favorite place.** A beach party, a Paris dinner or a menu from the city where they lived in their twenties.

## What games can you play at a 30th birthday party?

Play games about the birthday person: a quiz about their life, a baby photo round or two truths and a lie. They work for 10 guests or 60, and they give friends from different parts of their life something to laugh about together.

- **How well do you know the birthday person.** Fifteen to twenty questions, from their first job to their worst haircut.
- **Baby photo match.** Collect baby photos of the guests and guess who's who. The birthday person's photo goes last.
- **Two truths and a lie.** Each guest tells three stories about the birthday person, and the room guesses the lie.
- **Name that tune.** Two seconds of each song from their college years.
- **Who said it.** Read out lines from their old texts or posts, and guests guess the year.
- **Twenties superlatives.** Vote on "most likely to move abroad" or "best dance moves", with small prizes.

> **Example scenario (not a real event):** A 30th at home for 25 friends. The quiz asks what the birthday girl studied before she switched majors. Her college roommate says "marine biology". Her coworkers have no idea. Her younger brother answers "she wanted to be a DJ", and she admits he's right. The roommate still wins the quiz by one point, and the brother gets a cheer anyway.

## How do you make a 30th about their story?

Put their twenties on the screen: photos from every year, a few short videos from friends far away, and a game in which every guest answers questions about their life. Keep the toasts short, two or three minutes each.

Pick two or three speakers from different parts of their life, and let the game do the rest. It gives college friends, coworkers and family a reason to talk to each other, and the birthday person gets to react to every answer.
