Fifty years of marriage means a wedding in the 1970s, homes in more than one place, children, maybe grandchildren, and more stories than anyone can count. A 50th anniversary party brings together the people who were there for all of it. Here are 30 ideas, from a dinner at home to a family weekend away.

## What do most couples do for their 50th wedding anniversary?

Most couples celebrate a 50th with a party or dinner planned by their children, a trip, or both. The party usually looks back on their life together, with wedding photos, family stories and a toast.

1. **A family dinner** in a private room at a favorite restaurant.
2. **An open house** at home or at a child's house, from afternoon to evening.
3. **A trip** back to where they honeymooned or first met.
4. **A family weekend** at a lake house or a beach rental with the children and grandchildren.
5. **A vow renewal** followed by a reception for family and friends.

## Who throws a 50th anniversary party?

The couple's children usually host a 50th anniversary party, often with help from grandchildren or close friends. The couple can also host it themselves, which is common when the family lives far apart.

There is no fixed rule. What matters is that the couple wants a party and has a say in its size. Some couples love a big celebration, while others would prefer a quiet dinner and a trip.

## How do you plan a 50th anniversary party?

Start three to four months ahead: agree on the style with the couple, set a date and guest list, book a place, and send invitations six to eight weeks before. Collect photos and stories from family early, since they take the longest.

6. **Ask the couple** what they want: big or small, surprise or not, home or out.
7. **Set the guest list:** family, old friends, neighbors and, if possible, people from their wedding.
8. **Choose the place** and book it.
9. **Send invitations** six to eight weeks ahead. Long-distance relatives need more notice.
10. **Collect photos** from every decade and scan the old ones.
11. **Plan one shared moment:** toasts, a slideshow or a game.

## What are 50th anniversary party ideas at home?

At home, a 50th works well as an open house, a garden party or a dinner cooked from family recipes. Add a photo wall from five decades and a guest book, and the house tells their story for you.

12. **Open house** with finger food, so friends can come and go.
13. **Garden party** with lawn games for the grandchildren.
14. **Family recipe dinner:** dishes from the recipes they cooked for 50 years.
15. **Photo wall** with one panel per decade.
16. **Wedding album table** with their original album and the wedding dress or veil, if it survived.
17. **Recreate the wedding cake** from their wedding photos.
18. **Guest book with prompts:** "My favorite memory of you two is..."
19. **A playlist** with their wedding song and the music of each decade.

## What are fun things to do at a 50th anniversary party?

Fun activities at a 50th anniversary are about the couple: a game where they answer questions about each other, a guessing game with old photos and a memory jar. Each one gives guests a reason to share stories.

20. **The newlywed game, 50 years later.** The couple answers questions about each other back to back. Our [newlywed game questions](/blog/newlywed-game-questions/) work just as well after 50 years.
21. **Guess the year.** Guests guess the year of photos from their life together.
22. **Advice for the next 50 years.** Grandchildren ask the couple for marriage advice and read the answers aloud.
23. **Memory jar.** Each guest writes one memory, and the couple reads them the next morning.
24. **First dance, again.** They dance to their wedding song, and other couples join by length of marriage.
25. **Wedding trivia.** Questions about their wedding day: the date, the place, what went wrong.

## What food do you serve at a 50th anniversary party?

Serve food that fits the style: finger food for an open house, a buffet or plated dinner for a sit-down party. Add one dish from their wedding menu or a family recipe, and a cake in gold and white.

26. **The wedding menu, again,** if anyone remembers it.
27. **Family recipes** cooked by the children and grandchildren.
28. **A dessert table** in gold and white, with the couple's favorite sweets.

## What is the traditional gift for 50 years of marriage?

Gold is the traditional gift and the theme for a 50th wedding anniversary. It doesn't need to be solid gold: gold-rimmed glasses, a framed photo with gold trim or yellow roses all fit.

The Knot lists [gold as both the traditional and the modern 50th anniversary gift](https://www.theknot.com/content/50-year-anniversary-gift-ideas), with yellow roses as a flower for the year. Ideas guests and family give:

29. **A framed wedding photo** next to a new family photo, both in gold frames.
30. **A memory book** with letters from children, grandchildren and friends.

## What percentage of married couples make it to 50 years?

Only a small share of current marriages have reached 50 years: about 6 percent in Census Bureau data from 2009, and 7.7 percent in a 2022 analysis. That makes a golden anniversary a rare reason to celebrate.

The Census Bureau reported that [about 6 percent of currently married couples had reached their 50th anniversary](https://www.census.gov/newsroom/releases/archives/marital_status_living_arrangements/cb11-90.html) in 2009. Using the 2022 American Community Survey, Bowling Green State University found that [7.7 percent of current marriages had reached the 50-year mark](https://www.bgsu.edu/ncfmr/resources/data/family-profiles/loo-long-term-marriages-among-older-us-adults-2022-fp-24-01.html), and among married older adults, close to half had.

## How do you make a 50th anniversary special?

Make it about their story: collect memories from family and friends, show photos from every decade, and give the couple a moment to hear what they mean to everyone. Small personal touches matter more than the size of the party.
