Fifty is half a century of stories: the house they grew up in, the friends from every decade, the kids, the jobs, the trips. A 50th party is the rare night when all those people are in one room. Here are 30 ideas, from a dinner at home to a weekend away, plus decade themes, what to bring and what to say.

## What do people usually do for their 50th birthday?

Most people celebrate a 50th with a party or a dinner for family and friends, a trip, or both. The party usually looks back on their life, with photos, toasts and the people from every decade.

Common choices:

1. **A big party** with everyone they know, at home or in a rented space.
2. **A dinner** in a private room with close family and friends.
3. **A trip** with their partner or a few close friends, sometimes with a small party when they get back.
4. **A weekend with the family** at a lake house or a beach rental.
5. **A bucket-list day:** a hot air balloon, a cooking class with a chef, or a concert they've always wanted to see.

## What are 50th birthday party ideas at home?

At home, a 50th works best as a dinner party, a backyard party or an open house that guests drop into. Add a photo wall from five decades and one shared moment, like toasts or a game.

6. **Backyard party** with a caterer or a food truck.
7. **Dinner party** with the birthday person's favorite menu.
8. **Open house** from afternoon to evening, so friends with kids and older relatives can come at different times.
9. **Photo wall:** five panels, one per decade.
10. **Memory jar:** every guest writes a memory, and the birthday person reads them the next morning.
11. **Wine tasting** with a bottle from their birth year, if you can find one.
12. **Family recipe dinner:** dishes from the recipes they grew up on.
13. **Garden party** with lawn games for every age.
14. **Game night** about the birthday person, with questions from their 50 years.
15. **Fire pit evening** for a small group of old friends.

## What are 50th birthday party ideas for a man and for a woman?

Plan around their personality and their life, not their gender. Think about what they've always wanted to do, who they want to see, and how much they enjoy being the center of attention.

16. **Bucket-list experience:** a race car day, a hot air balloon or a cooking class.
17. **Weekend away** with their oldest friends.
18. **Golf day** with a dinner after.
19. **Spa weekend** with a sibling or friends.
20. **Dinner in a private room** at the restaurant where they got engaged or celebrated a big moment.
21. **Concert night** with the music they loved at 20.
22. **Sports day:** bowling, pickleball or a softball game with friends and family.
23. **Boat day** on a lake or the coast.

## What are fun group activities for a 50th birthday party?

Choose activities that mix generations and circles: a quiz about the birthday person, a decade dance-off or a photo game. Each one gives guests who've never met a reason to talk.

24. **"How well do you know the birthday person" quiz.** Questions from all five decades. Our [100 how well do you know me questions](/blog/how-well-do-you-know-me-questions/) are a good start.
25. **Guess the year.** Show photos of the birthday person, and guests guess the year each was taken.
26. **Decade dance-off.** The DJ plays one song from each decade of their life, and a different table leads each one.
27. **Toast circle.** Each guest says one sentence about the birthday person.
28. **Time capsule.** Guests write a letter to the birthday person to open at 60.
29. **Lawn games tournament:** cornhole, bocce or horseshoes, in teams that mix family and friends.
30. **Karaoke from their teenage years.**

## What is a good theme for a 50th?

The classic theme is the decade they were born in, or the decade they were a teenager. Someone turning 50 in 2027 was born in 1977, so a late-1970s or 1980s party fits.

A few facts that make a decade party feel real: the Rubik's Cube was [sold around the world by 1980](https://www.britannica.com/topic/puzzle), and the [first Walkman went on sale in Japan on July 1, 1979](https://www.history.com/this-day-in-history/July-1/the-first-sony-walkman-goes-on-sale). Use the decade's look, music styles and food, and avoid printing real celebrities' faces on the decor.

| Decade | Decor | Music | Food | Game idea |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1970s | Disco ball, shag rugs, orange and brown | Disco, soul, soft rock | Fondue, deviled eggs | Guess the year from baby photos |
| 1980s | Neon, cassettes, puzzle cubes | Synth pop, new wave | Pizza bagels, punch | Name that tune from their teens |
| 1990s | Denim, flannel, butterfly clips | Grunge, R&B, pop | Pizza, soda floats | Trivia about their college years |
| Their life | Photos from all five decades | A playlist from each decade | Their favorite meals | A quiz about their life |

Other themes that work: "Fabulous at 50" in gold and black, a vineyard dinner, or a party in the style of the city where they grew up.

## What should you bring to a 50th birthday party?

Bring a card with a specific memory, and if you want, a gift that fits their life now. If the invitation says "no gifts", the card with a memory is still welcome.

Ideas that people keep:

- **A card with a memory.** One story from your years together means more than a long poem.
- **An old photo of the two of you,** printed and framed.
- **Something for their next chapter:** a book, a plant, a class or an experience you do together.
- **A bottle from their birth year,** if they enjoy wine.
- **A contribution to the party:** help with setup, a dish, or a story for the game.

## What is a good saying or toast for a 50th birthday?

A good 50th toast tells one specific story, says what the person means to the room and ends with a short line. Keep it to two or three minutes.

[Toastmasters suggests keeping a toast to two or three minutes](https://www.toastmasters.org/resources/public-speaking-tips/how-to-give-a-toast). Short sayings that work as the last line, on a cake or on a card:

- "Fifty years of stories, and the best chapter starts tonight."
- "Here's to the first half. The second half is going to be even better."
- "Not over the hill, just at the top of it."
- "Fifty looks good on you."
- "To fifty years of [name], and the fifty people who can't imagine life without you."

## How can guests share 50 years of stories?

Collect stories from guests before the party, and turn them into a slideshow, a memory book or a game in which everyone guesses which story belongs to which decade. Friends who can't come can send a video.
