A bridal shower brings together people from every part of the bride's life: her college roommates, her coworkers, her future in-laws and her grandmother. The right game gives them something to share in the first ten minutes. Here are 25 bridal shower games that guests enjoy, sorted by the group you have and the mood you want.

## How many games are normal for a bridal shower?

Two or three games are normal for a shower of about three hours. One game is enough for a short brunch, and more than four starts to feel like a schedule.

A planner quoted by The Knot calls [three hours or less the sweet spot for a bridal shower](https://www.theknot.com/content/how-long-are-bridal-showers). In that time you also have a meal, gift opening and time to talk, so the games get about 30 to 45 minutes in all.

A plan that works for most showers:

1. **As guests arrive:** a game people can play on their own, like bridal bingo or the advice cards.
2. **After the meal:** the main game, usually about the bride.
3. **Before the gifts or at the end:** one short, funny game to finish on a laugh.

## What are fun bridal shower games for a small group?

For 15 guests or fewer, choose games that let people talk and tell stories. A quiz about the bride, two truths and a lie, and whose memory is it all work around one table.

**1. How well do you know the bride.** The host asks questions about the bride, everyone writes an answer, and the bride reveals the right one. We have [75 ready-made bridal shower trivia questions](/blog/bridal-shower-trivia-questions/) for it.

**2. What did the groom say.** Before the shower, ask the groom 10 to 15 questions about the bride and record his answers, on paper or on video. At the shower the bride guesses each answer before you reveal it.

**3. Two truths and a lie about the bride.** Each guest tells three short stories about the bride, and the room guesses which one never happened. It's also the fastest way for guests from different circles to meet.

**4. Advice cards.** Every guest writes one serious piece of advice and one funny one for the couple. The bride reads some aloud and keeps all of them.

**5. Recipe card swap.** Send a blank recipe card with the invitation, and each guest brings a favorite recipe. The bride guesses who brought which dish, and ends the day with a cookbook from the people she loves.

**6. Whose memory is it?** Each guest writes a memory with the bride on a slip of paper, without a name. The bride reads them one by one and guesses who wrote each.

## What are easy bridal shower games for a big group?

With 20 guests or more, choose games people can play from their seats or on their own, with no turns to wait for. Bingo, a purse hunt and don't say "wedding" keep a big room busy with little effort.

**7. Bridal shower bingo.** Each square has a gift the bride might open: a mixing bowl, towels, a picture frame. Guests mark squares during gift opening, and the first full row wins.

**8. What's in your purse?** The host reads a list of items, from a receipt to a bandage, and guests score a point for each one in their bag. The strangest item earns a bonus point.

**9. What's on your phone?** Like the purse game, with phones: a photo of a pet, more than 20 unread emails, a selfie from this morning. Points for each.

**10. Don't say "wedding."** Every guest gets a clothespin. Whoever hears someone say "wedding" takes their clothespin, and whoever has the most at the end wins.

**11. Guess how many kisses.** Fill a jar with chocolate kisses, and guests write their guess on a card. The closest guess takes the jar home.

**12. Pass the bouquet.** Guests pass a bouquet while music plays. When the music stops, whoever holds it takes a note from the bouquet and answers the question or does the task on it.

**13. Find the guest who.** A sheet of squares like "has known the bride since grade school" or "has been to the couple's favorite restaurant". Guests walk around and find someone for each square.

## What are funny bridal shower games?

Funny games get guests moving or making things up: a toilet paper wedding dress, a fill in the blanks love story, acting out the proposal. Keep them silly, not embarrassing, especially with family in the room.

**14. Toilet paper wedding dress.** Teams design a wedding dress out of toilet paper on one teammate in five minutes. The bride picks the winner.

**15. Would she rather.** The host reads a choice, like "a beach honeymoon or a city honeymoon", and guests vote on what the bride would choose. Then the bride answers.

**16. Fill in the blanks love story.** Write the story of how the couple met with blanks for nouns, adjectives and places. Guests call out words without seeing the story, and the host reads the result.

**17. Act out the proposal.** Small teams have two minutes to rehearse the couple's proposal story, then perform it. The bride judges accuracy, the room judges drama.

**18. Bride charades.** Cards with moments from the couple's life: the first date, the first apartment, the dog's first day home. Teams act them out without words.

## What bridal shower games are about the bride?

The best games use the bride's own photos, trips and story, so every answer teaches the guests something new about her. They take some preparation, but they're the ones guests talk about afterward.

**19. How old was she?** Show 10 to 15 photos of the bride from childhood to today, and guests guess her age in each. Her mom and grandmother usually win this one.

**20. Where in the world?** Photos from the couple's trips, and guests guess the place. Add a bonus point for the year.

**21. Love story timeline.** Hand out cards with moments from the relationship, like the first date, the first trip and the proposal. Teams race to put them in the right order.

**22. Family vs friends.** The bride's family plays against her friends, with questions about the bride from every stage of her life. Each side knows things the other doesn't.

## What games work at a couples shower?

At a couples shower, choose games where both partners play and both families compete. The couples newlywed game, two families trivia and a date night jar include everyone.

**23. The couples newlywed game.** The couple answers questions about each other, sitting back to back. You can run it like the wedding shoe game, with our [60 shoe game questions](/blog/wedding-shoe-game-questions/).

**24. Two families trivia.** The bride's side and the groom's side answer questions about both partners. It's the fastest way for two families to learn each other's stories.

**25. Date night jar.** Every guest or couple writes a date idea on a card and drops it in a jar. The couple takes the jar home and pulls one out every month.

## How do you make a bridal shower actually fun?

Build the shower around the bride's personality, keep the games short, and leave plenty of time to eat and talk. A shower is fun when guests feel part of the bride's story, not like an audience to a schedule.

- **Ask the bride what she enjoys.** Some brides love being the center of a quiz, others would rather watch.
- **Mix the guests early.** An icebreaker game in the first 20 minutes makes the rest of the day easier.
- **Keep each game under 15 minutes.** End while people still want more.
- **Make gift opening shorter.** Play bingo during it, or open gifts in small groups.
- **Know who hosts.** The Knot's [guide for shower hosts](https://www.theknot.com/content/bridal-shower-host-etiquette) says the maid of honor, the bridal party or the mother of the bride traditionally host, and that the bride herself shouldn't, since a shower is a gift-giving event for her.

## What can you do instead of games at a bridal shower?

Choose one shared activity instead: a workshop, a brunch with toasts, or a live quiz about the bride on a screen. Guests still get a reason to talk, without anyone being put on the spot.

Some ideas:

- **A workshop:** flower arranging, candle making or a cooking class.
- **A toast circle:** each guest shares a short wish or memory, and someone records it for the bride.
- **A memory book:** guests bring a photo with the bride and write a page next to it.
- **A tea party or brunch** with a long table and no agenda.
- **A live game on the screen.** All the guests answer questions about the bride from their phones, and the screen shows who knows her best.
