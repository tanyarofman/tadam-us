## What are Christmas family games with gifts and prizes?

Gift games work best when every gift is small and everyone brings one: a white elephant swap, a dice game where a six lets you steal, or a ball of plastic wrap with prizes inside. Set a spending limit so nobody feels awkward.

**2. Dice gift steal.** Put all the wrapped gifts in the middle. Players take turns rolling a die: a 6 lets you take a gift from the pile, and once the pile is empty, a 6 lets you steal one from anyone. Set a timer for 10 minutes, and whatever you're holding when it rings is yours.

**3. Pass the present.** Wrap a small prize in many layers of paper, with a candy between each layer. Pass it around while music plays, and whoever holds it when the music stops removes one layer. The person who opens the last layer keeps the prize.

**4. Plastic wrap ball.** Roll small prizes into a big ball of plastic wrap. One player unwraps as fast as they can and keeps every prize that falls out, while the player to their left rolls a pair of dice. When that player rolls doubles, the ball passes to them.

## How do you play the white elephant gift game?

Everyone brings one wrapped gift, draws a number, and on their turn either opens a new gift or steals one already opened. A gift can be stolen a set number of times, usually three, and then it's safe.

The name comes from the phrase for a gift that costs more trouble than it's worth: [Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/white%20elephant) defines a white elephant as something that needs much care and expense and gives little back. That's why the funniest gifts are often the best ones.

1. Set a price limit and a theme if you like, such as "something you'd regift" or "something homemade".
2. Place every wrapped gift on one table.
3. Everyone draws a number.
4. Player 1 opens a gift and shows it to the room.
5. Each next player either opens a new gift or steals an opened one. Someone who loses a gift picks again, and can't steal it straight back.
6. After the third steal, a gift is frozen.
7. When everyone has a gift, player 1 gets one last chance to swap.

For kids, skip the stealing and play a simple swap, so nobody ends the day in tears over a gift.

## What are Christmas games for families with kids?

Kids want to move, so pick active games with short rounds: a reindeer ring toss, a sock snowball fight into a bucket, freeze dance to Christmas music and an ornament hunt. Keep each game under 10 minutes and give every child a small prize.

- **5. Reindeer ring toss.** One adult wears paper antlers, and the kids toss paper plate rings onto them from a line.
- **6. Pin the nose on the snowman.** Draw a big snowman, cut out an orange paper nose and use a scarf as the blindfold.
- **7. Sock snowball toss.** Roll white socks into balls and throw them into a laundry basket. Move the basket back after every round.
- **8. Freeze dance.** Play Christmas music and pause it. Anyone still moving is out, or does a reindeer pose to get back in.
- **9. Ornament hunt.** Hide one special ornament in the tree. The first child to spot it opens the first gift.
- **21. Candy cane hunt.** Hide candy canes around the house like eggs at Easter, with a few hard ones for the older kids.

## What are Christmas games for family with adults?

Adults want games that make them laugh at each other: a gift-wrap race in oven mitts, carol charades, guessing baby photos and naming the year of an old family photo. Mixed teams let grandparents and teenagers win together.

- **11. Oven mitt wrap race.** Teams race to wrap a box neatly while wearing oven mitts. Others judge the result.
- **12. Carol charades.** Write carol titles on slips of paper and act them out without words. "Silent Night" is a gift.
- **13. Guess the baby photo.** Ask everyone to send a baby photo ahead of time and number them on a board.
- **16. Name that year.** Show old family Christmas photos and let teams guess the year from the clothes, the hair and the tree.
- **14. Christmas trivia.** A round of 10 questions between dinner and dessert. Take them from our [60 Christmas trivia questions](/blog/christmas-trivia-questions/).
- **15. Who knows the family best.** Ten questions about the people at the table, such as who cried at their own birthday party. Start from our [how well do you know me questions](/blog/how-well-do-you-know-me-questions/).

> **Example scenario (not a real event):** Fourteen people at a grandmother's house, ages 4 to 81. The oven mitt wrap race goes to the grandfather and his 9 year old grandson, who wins on neatness. In "name that year", nobody beats the aunt who remembers every Christmas sweater since 1994.

## What are Christmas games with candy canes and cups?

Quick 60 second challenges are the easiest family games: hook candy canes with a candy cane held in your mouth, stack 36 cups into a tree, or scoop cotton balls blindfolded. Each round takes a minute, so everyone gets several turns.

- **17. Candy cane hook race.** Hold a candy cane in your mouth by the straight end and use the hook to pick up other candy canes from the table, without hands.
- **18. Cup tower.** Stack plastic cups into a pyramid shaped like a tree (8, 7, 6 and down to 1 makes 36 cups) in 60 seconds.
- **19. Cotton ball scoop.** Blindfolded, scoop cotton balls from one bowl to another with a spoon. Count them after a minute.
- **20. Ornament bounce.** Bounce a ping pong ball off the table into a row of cups. Each cup is worth more points the farther it is.

Play in teams of two, one young and one old, and keep a score sheet on the fridge.

## How do you play the 5 second Christmas game?

One player reads a category, like "three things you hang on a tree", and the next player names three answers before 5 seconds run out. Make it fair with easier categories for kids and harder ones for adults.

Categories that work:

- Three Christmas carols
- Three things in a stocking
- Three things that are red and green
- Three Christmas foods from other countries
- Three things you'd find at the North Pole
- Three gifts from "The Twelve Days of Christmas"

Count 5 seconds out loud or use a phone timer. Every right answer in time is a point, and the player with the most points after three rounds wins.

## What are the 12 games of Christmas?

The 12 games of Christmas is a family tournament of 12 short games, one for each of the twelve days. Play them all in one evening, or one game a day from Christmas to Epiphany on January 6.

The [twelve days of Christmas](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Twelve-Days-of-Christmas) start on Christmas Day and lead up to Epiphany on January 6, so a game a day keeps the fun going through the school break. A good set of 12:

1. Candy cane hook race
2. Cup tower
3. Carol charades
4. Sock snowball toss
5. Oven mitt wrap race
6. Cotton ball scoop
7. 5 second game
8. Ornament bounce
9. Plate drawing: draw a Christmas scene on a paper plate held on top of your head
10. Name that year
11. Christmas trivia
12. Who knows the family best

Keep a scoreboard on the fridge and give the champion a crown made from wrapping paper. If the tournament runs to December 31, finish with our [New Year's Eve games](/blog/new-years-eve-games/).
