An engagement party is often the first time the two families, and the two groups of friends, are in one room. A good game gives them something to laugh about together, without turning the party into a show. Here are 20 engagement party games, for the couple and for the guests, and how to keep the evening easy.

## Do you play games at an engagement party?

You don't have to, but one short game helps two families and two groups of friends mix. Keep it light and optional: a trivia round about the couple or a guest bingo works better than a full program of party games.

Most engagement parties are cocktail parties, dinners or backyard barbecues, with no fixed schedule. One game in the middle of the party, right before or after the toasts, gives the evening a center. The rest of the time, an activity guests can join at their own pace keeps the room moving.

## What are fun engagement party games?

The most fun engagement party games are about the couple and easy to join: how well do you know the couple, the how-they-met timeline, find-the-guest bingo and a wedding guessing game. Choose one main game and one or two activities from the table.

A few notes:

- **Find the guest bingo (3).** Each square is a fact about a guest ("was the couple's first roommate", "flew in from abroad"). Guests find the people who match, so the two families meet.
- **Wedding guessing game (4).** Guests guess the first dance song, the honeymoon spot and who will cry first. Open the ballots at the wedding.
- **Proposal story quiz (16).** Questions about the proposal itself: where, what was said, and who knew in advance.
- **Map of their story (11).** A map with pins for where they met, their first trip and where the wedding will be.

## What engagement party games are there for the couple?

The best couple games are the shoe game, a "what did they say" round and two truths and a lie. Each partner answers questions about the other, and the guests see who really knows whom.

- **Shoe game (5).** The couple sits back to back, each holding one of their own shoes and one of their partner's, and raises a shoe to answer "who is more likely to…". Save the full version for the reception, and play five or six questions here.
- **What did they say (6).** Before the party, ask each partner 10 questions about the other. At the party, the other partner guesses, then the real answer is revealed.
- **Two truths and a lie (7).** Each partner tells three stories about the relationship, and the room guesses the lie.

## What engagement party games work for large groups?

For 50 guests or more, choose games everyone plays at once from their seats or while they mingle: couple trivia in teams, find-the-guest bingo, the wedding guessing game or a who-is-more-likely round with paddles.

- **Couple trivia in teams (1).** Tables or groups of six, one answer sheet each.
- **Find the guest bingo (3).** The bigger the group, the better it works.
- **Who is more likely (8).** Every guest gets a paddle with the couple's photos and votes on each question.
- **Name that love song (13).** Two seconds of each song, teams shout the answer.

Avoid games that need volunteers on a stage: in a big group, most guests just watch.

## How do you entertain guests at an engagement party?

Give guests a reason to talk to each other: name tags with how they know the couple, an activity they can join at their own pace, one short game and a few toasts. Good food and a good playlist do the rest.

1. **Name tags with a line.** "Sarah, the bride's college roommate." It's the easiest icebreaker for two families.
2. **One activity from the start.** Advice cards, a map of their story or a photo booth.
3. **One game in the middle.** About 15 minutes, everyone in the room.
4. **Short toasts.** A parent from each side and one friend, two minutes each.
5. **Keep the guest list in line with the wedding.** The Knot calls it the [golden etiquette rule](https://www.theknot.com/content/whos-invited-to-engagement-party) that everyone invited to the engagement party should be invited to the wedding.

> **Example scenario (not a real event):** An engagement party for 60 guests in a backyard. The trivia round asks what the groom said right after the bride said yes. Her side guesses "finally". His side guesses "I love you". The real answer is "wait, I dropped the ring", and both families laugh at the same moment, which is exactly what the hosts hoped for.

## What prizes work for engagement party games?

Small, shareable prizes work best: a bottle of wine, a box of chocolates, a candle or a plant. Keep the prizes modest, since the focus is on the couple, and let winners choose from a basket.

- **A prize basket.** Winners choose in order.
- **Something to share.** Wine or chocolate the winners can open at the party.
- **A wedding-themed prize.** The winner gets the first dance with the couple at the wedding, or a reserved seat at the rehearsal dinner.
