A family reunion brings together people who share a last name but sometimes not much else: cousins who haven't met since they were kids, in-laws, babies and great-grandparents. Games give them a reason to talk, laugh and get to know each other. Here are 30 family reunion games for all ages, indoors and out.

## What games do you play at a family reunion?

The most popular family reunion games are family trivia, relay races, tug of war, a photo guessing game and lawn games like cornhole. The best ones put different generations on the same team.

1. **Family trivia.** Questions about the family's history, places and stories.
2. **Guess the baby photo.** Relatives send baby photos ahead, and everyone guesses who is who.
3. **Family tree puzzle.** Teams put names on a blank family tree, and the first team to finish correctly wins.
4. **Who am I.** A relative's name goes on each person's back, and they ask yes or no questions to guess it.

## What are family reunion games for all ages?

Games for all ages are simple, short and don't depend on strength or speed: trivia, scavenger hunts, bingo and guessing games. Pair the youngest with the oldest, and everyone has a job.

5. **Family bingo.** Squares like "born in another state", "has twins" or "plays an instrument". Players find a relative who fits each square.
6. **Scavenger hunt** with a list of things to find or photograph around the venue.
7. **Two truths and a lie, family edition.** Each person shares three facts, and relatives vote on the lie.
8. **Name that relative.** Read a fun fact, and teams guess who it's about.
9. **Pass the story.** One person starts a story, and each person adds one sentence.

## What are outdoor family reunion games?

Outdoor family reunion games are classic picnic games: sack races, tug of war, a water balloon toss and lawn games. Play them in the morning or late afternoon, when it's cooler.

10. **Sack race** with pillowcases or burlap sacks.
11. **Tug of war,** branch against branch.
12. **Water balloon toss** in pairs.
13. **Three-legged race.**
14. **Cornhole tournament** with a bracket on a poster board.
15. **Egg and spoon race.**
16. **Kickball,** with grandparents as pitchers.

In July heat, keep an eye on the youngest and oldest players. The CDC advises [limiting outdoor activity in the hottest part of the day](https://www.cdc.gov/climate-health/php/resources/protect-yourself-from-the-dangers-of-extreme-heat.html), resting in the shade and drinking water often, and notes that [older adults are more sensitive to heat](https://www.cdc.gov/heat-health/risk-factors/heat-and-older-adults-aged-65.html).

## What are indoor family reunion games?

Indoor family reunion games work in a hall, a rental house or a living room: trivia on a TV, charades, a memory jar and board games. They're also the backup plan if it rains.

17. **Family charades** with scenes from family stories.
18. **Memory jar.** Each person writes a favorite family memory, and they're read aloud at dinner.
19. **Board game tables** with a different game at each table.
20. **Family trivia on the TV.** For ready questions, use our [100 family trivia questions](/blog/family-trivia-questions/).

## Which family reunion games work for large groups?

For large groups, choose games that everyone can play at once: trivia in teams, bingo, a scavenger hunt or a relay. Use a microphone or a big screen, so the whole room can follow.

| Game | Where | Ages | Group |
|---|---|---|---|
| Family trivia | Indoor | All | 10 to 200 |
| Family bingo | Both | All | Any |
| Scavenger hunt | Outdoor | 6 and up | Teams |
| Tug of war | Outdoor | 8 and up | 10 to 40 |
| Sack race | Outdoor | Kids, teens | 4 to 20 |
| Cornhole | Outdoor | All | 2 to 16 |
| Baby photo guess | Both | All | Any |
| Memory jar | Indoor | All | Any |

21. **Relay race** with mixed teams and a different task for each leg.
22. **Family Olympics:** five short games, points for each team, and medals at the end.

## What are funny family reunion games for adults?

Funny games for adults are about family stories and embarrassing photos: "Who said it", a "guess the year" photo game and the newlywed game for long-married couples. Nothing makes a reunion laugh like an old haircut.

23. **Who said it.** Read famous family quotes, and teams guess who said them.
24. **Guess the year.** Old family photos on a screen, and teams guess the year.
25. **The newlywed game for couples** who have been married the longest.
26. **Worst family vacation.** Each branch tells its worst trip, and everyone votes.
27. **Bad family recipe contest:** the dish nobody wants to admit they used to love.

> **Example scenario (not a real event):** A reunion of 65 relatives in a park pavilion. In "Who said it", the quote "I'm not lost, I'm exploring" goes to the whole room. Half the teams guess the grandfather. The answer is his wife, on the 1998 road trip when she was driving. Three cousins who were in the back seat stand up to tell the rest.

## What are good family reunion activities besides games?

Besides games, family reunions often include a group photo, a family history display, a talent show, a shared meal and a memory book. Plan one activity that brings the whole family together.

28. **Family history wall** with old photos, a family tree and a map of where everyone lives.
29. **Group photo** with everyone in matching colors or T-shirts.
30. **Talent show** for the kids and the brave adults.

Other ideas: a family recipe cookbook, interviews with the oldest relatives, and a time capsule to open at the next reunion.

## What prizes work for family reunion games?

Good family reunion prizes are small and fun: ribbons, a traveling trophy, candy, or the right to choose the next reunion's menu. A trophy that passes to the winners at each reunion becomes a tradition.

- **A traveling trophy** with the winners' names added each year.
- **Ribbons or medals** for each game.
- **Funny prizes:** the "longest trip to get here" award or the "most new family members" award.
- **Bragging rights,** printed on a certificate.
