Family trivia is one of the few games a grandmother, a teenager and a seven-year-old can play together. The secret is the mix: easy questions for kids, harder ones for adults and a round about the family that nobody else could answer. Here are 100 family trivia questions with answers, sorted into rounds.

## What is family trivia, and how do you play it?

Family trivia is a quiz game for players of all ages, usually in teams that mix kids and adults. One person reads the questions, teams agree on an answer, and the team with the most points wins.

Play it after dinner, on a road trip or at a family reunion. A plan for one hour:

| Round | Best for | Questions | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kids | Under 12 | 10 | Spider legs |
| Grown-ups | Teens, adults | 10 | Capital of Australia |
| All ages | Everyone | 10 | Hours in a week |
| Funny facts | Everyone | 10 | Giraffe tongue |
| Our family | Everyone | 10 | Who was born where |

Read each question twice, give 30 seconds to answer, and read the answers after each round.
