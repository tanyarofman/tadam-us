A good trivia question is one where half the room is sure of the answer and the other half is sure of a different one. Here are 100 trivia questions for adults with answers, sorted into rounds from easy to hard, so you can build a whole trivia night in ten minutes.

## How do you run a trivia night with these questions?

Pick four or five rounds, eight to ten questions each, and split players into teams of four to six. Read each question twice, give 30 seconds to answer, and score after every round.

The format comes from the pub quiz, which took off in Britain after [a company called Burns and Porter organized quiz leagues in pubs in the 1970s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pub_quiz). What works in a pub works at home or at the office: teams, short rounds and a small prize. Use the table to choose your rounds.

| Round | Questions | Level | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Easy warm-up | 15 | Easy | Everyone |
| Geography | 15 | Mid | Adults |
| History | 15 | Mid | Adults |
| Science and nature | 15 | Mid | Mixed ages |
| Food and drink | 12 | Easy | Everyone |
| Sports and games | 10 | Easy | Everyone |
| Hard questions | 10 | Hard | Trivia fans |
| Funny facts | 8 | Mid | Last round |

A few tips from hosts who run trivia often:

- **Ban phones** or set a 30-second limit per question.
- **Mix the teams.** Put people who don't know each other on the same team.
- **Keep the last round surprising,** so the scores can still change.

## What are easy trivia questions to warm up?

Start with questions almost everyone can answer, so every team scores in round one. Easy questions get people talking and settle the nerves of anyone who thinks they're bad at trivia.

1. **How many continents are there?** Seven.
2. **What is the largest ocean on Earth?** The Pacific.
3. **How many sides does a hexagon have?** Six.
4. **What color do you get when you mix blue and yellow?** Green.
5. **How many days are in a leap year?** 366.
6. **At what temperature in Fahrenheit does water freeze?** 32°F.
7. **Which planet is called the Red Planet?** Mars.
8. **How many legs does a spider have?** Eight.
9. **What is the tallest animal in the world?** The giraffe.
10. **How many strings does a standard guitar have?** Six.
11. **In which country are the pyramids of Giza?** Egypt.
12. **What is the main ingredient in guacamole?** Avocado.
13. **How many minutes are in a day?** 1,440.
14. **What is the hardest natural substance?** Diamond.
15. **What do caterpillars turn into?** Butterflies or moths.

## What are good geography trivia questions?

Good geography questions mix capitals people half remember with surprising facts about rivers, deserts and borders. Capitals that aren't the biggest city, like Canberra and Ottawa, catch a lot of teams.

16. **What is the capital of Australia?** Canberra, not Sydney.
17. **What is the longest river in the world?** The Nile, by most measurements. Some researchers argue for the Amazon.
18. **Which US state has the most people?** California.
19. **What is the largest country by area?** Russia.
20. **What is the smallest country in the world?** Vatican City.
21. **Which US state is made up entirely of islands?** Hawaii.
22. **What is the capital of Canada?** Ottawa.
23. **Which mountain range is the traditional border between Europe and Asia?** The Ural Mountains.
24. **What is the largest desert in the world?** Antarctica. The Sahara is the largest hot desert.
25. **Which river flows through Paris?** The Seine.
26. **In which country is the city of Istanbul?** Turkey (Türkiye).
27. **What is the highest mountain above sea level?** Mount Everest.
28. **In which US state is the Grand Canyon?** Arizona.
29. **Which two countries share the longest international border?** The United States and Canada.
30. **What is the capital of New Zealand?** Wellington.

## What are good history trivia questions?

Good history questions ask about dates and events most adults learned in school, plus a few that surprise. Mix US history with world history so everyone has a round they're good at.

31. **In what year was the Declaration of Independence adopted?** 1776.
32. **Who was the first president of the United States?** George Washington.
33. **In what year did Apollo 11 land on the Moon?** 1969.
34. **Which of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World is still standing?** The Great Pyramid of Giza.
35. **Which wall fell in 1989?** The Berlin Wall.
36. **Which ship sank in 1912 on its first voyage?** The Titanic.
37. **Who flew the first powered airplane, in 1903?** The Wright brothers.
38. **Which civilization built Machu Picchu?** The Inca.
39. **In what year did World War II end?** 1945.
40. **Which English document from 1215 limited the power of the king?** The Magna Carta.
41. **What was the name of the first artificial satellite, launched in 1957?** Sputnik 1.
42. **Which was the last state to join the United States?** Hawaii, in 1959.
43. **Which city was the first capital of the United States under the Constitution?** New York City.
44. **Which ancient civilization wrote in hieroglyphs?** The ancient Egyptians.
45. **What right did the 19th Amendment protect in 1920?** Women's right to vote.

## What are good science and nature trivia questions?

Good science questions have short answers that sound simple but make people hesitate: chemical symbols, body facts and animals. They also suit mixed ages, since kids often know them best.

46. **What is the chemical symbol for gold?** Au.
47. **What is the largest planet in the solar system?** Jupiter. According to [NASA](https://science.nasa.gov/jupiter/), it's more than twice as massive as all the other planets combined.
48. **Which gas do plants take in from the air?** Carbon dioxide.
49. **How many bones are in the adult human body?** 206.
50. **What is the closest star to Earth?** The Sun.
51. **What is the largest organ of the human body?** The skin.
52. **What is the largest mammal?** The blue whale.
53. **How many hearts does an octopus have?** Three.
54. **Which blood type is known as the universal donor?** O negative.
55. **What force keeps us on the ground?** Gravity.
56. **How many chambers does the human heart have?** Four.
57. **What does the symbol O stand for on the periodic table?** Oxygen.
58. **About how long does sunlight take to reach Earth?** About 8 minutes.
59. **What is the longest bone in the human body?** The femur, in the thigh.
60. **Which metal is liquid at room temperature?** Mercury.

## What are good food and drink trivia questions?

Food questions are the easiest round to love, because everyone has an opinion. Ask where dishes come from, what they're made of and a few botanical surprises.

61. **What is the main ingredient in hummus?** Chickpeas.
62. **Which nut is used to make marzipan?** Almonds.
63. **Which fruit is dried to make raisins?** Grapes.
64. **Which spice is the most expensive by weight?** Saffron.
65. **Which country does Gouda cheese come from?** The Netherlands.
66. **Which drink is made by fermenting honey?** Mead.
67. **Which fruit has its seeds on the outside?** The strawberry.
68. **What is the main ingredient in paella?** Rice.
69. **Is a tomato a fruit or a vegetable, botanically?** A fruit.
70. **Which vegetable are most American pickles made from?** Cucumbers.
71. **What is sushi rice seasoned with?** Rice vinegar, sugar and salt.
72. **In which country did the croissant's ancestor, the kipferl, come from?** Austria.

## What are good sports and games trivia questions?

Good sports questions ask about rules, numbers and games everyone has played, not about specific teams or players. That way the round works for fans and non-fans alike.

73. **How many players does a soccer team have on the field?** 11.
74. **How many points is a touchdown worth in American football?** Six.
75. **What is a perfect score in bowling?** 300.
76. **How many squares are on a chessboard?** 64.
77. **How often are the Summer Olympics held?** Every four years.
78. **In which sport does "love" mean zero?** Tennis.
79. **How many holes are on a standard golf course?** 18.
80. **How many dots are there on a standard six-sided die in total?** 21.
81. **How long is a marathon?** 26.2 miles.
82. **How many points is a basketball shot from behind the arc worth?** Three.

## What are the hardest trivia questions for adults?

The hardest trivia questions sound like you should know them, but the answer is a word or number few people remember. Save them for the last rounds, when teams are close.

83. **Which is the only letter that doesn't appear in any US state name?** Q.
84. **Which planet rotates on its side?** Uranus.
85. **How many time zones does Russia have?** 11.
86. **What is the smallest bone in the human body?** The stapes, in the middle ear.
87. **In what year did Constantinople fall to the Ottoman Empire?** 1453.
88. **Which language has the most native speakers?** Mandarin Chinese.
89. **Which planet has the shortest day?** Jupiter, about 10 hours.
90. **What is the dot over a lowercase i called?** A tittle.
91. **Which country's flag is not a rectangle?** Nepal.
92. **What is the most abundant gas in Earth's atmosphere?** Nitrogen.

## What are funny trivia questions with surprising answers?

Funny trivia questions have answers that make people laugh or argue, like animal facts and things that sound made up. They make a great last round.

93. **What is a group of flamingos called?** A flamboyance.
94. **Botanically, which is a berry: a banana or a strawberry?** The banana.
95. **What color is a polar bear's skin?** Black.
96. **Which is older: Oxford University or the Aztec city of Tenochtitlan?** Oxford. Teaching there began before 1100, more than two centuries before Tenochtitlan was founded.
97. **What is a baby kangaroo called?** A joey.
98. **Which animal has fingerprints so close to ours they're hard to tell apart?** The koala.
99. **What is a group of crows called?** A murder.
100. **How many eyes does a honeybee have?** Five: two large ones and three small ones on top of its head.

> **Example scenario (not a real event):** A neighborhood trivia night with six teams. Going into the funny round, the "Cul-de-Sac Crew" trails by four points. Question 93 asks what a group of flamingos is called, and a ten-year-old on the team whispers "flamboyance". They win by one point and she keeps the trophy on her desk for a year.

## What are good trivia questions about someone you know?

The best questions about someone are about stories only friends and family know: first jobs, childhood nicknames, travel mishaps. They turn a trivia night into a celebration of a person, and nobody can look up the answer.

Some ideas for a birthday, a retirement or a team party:

- What was their first job?
- Which city did they almost move to?
- What did they want to be at age 10?
- What's the most-played song on their phone?
- Which food can't they stand?
