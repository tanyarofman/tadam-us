A graduation party mixes people who rarely meet: grandparents, teachers, teammates, neighbors and the graduate's friends. Games give them something to do together, and they turn the party from small talk into stories about the graduate. Here are 25 graduation party games, for high school and college grads, indoors and out.

## What games do you play at a graduation party?

The most popular graduation party games are about the graduate: a photo guessing game, trivia about their school years and advice cards. Add a couple of lawn games for guests who want to move around.

About [3.7 million students are projected to finish high school in the 2026-27 school year](https://nces.ed.gov/programs/digest/d21/tables/dt21_219.10.asp), so in late May and June the same guests often go to several parties on one weekend. Games that guests can join in five minutes work better than a long program.

1. **Guess the year.** Show photos of the graduate from kindergarten to senior year, and guests guess the grade.
2. **Advice cards.** Guests write one piece of advice for the next chapter. The graduate reads them later.
3. **Prediction board.** Guests predict where the graduate will be in 10 years.
4. **Trivia about the graduate.** Questions about their school years, friends and funniest moments.

## What are graduation party games for high school grads?

High school graduation party games work best when friends and family both play: a quiz about the graduate's school years, a yearbook scavenger hunt and a photo booth with props. Keep them short and easy to join.

5. **Yearbook scavenger hunt.** Teams find answers in old yearbooks: the oldest teacher, the funniest club photo, the graduate's first school picture.
6. **Senior superlatives for guests.** Guests vote on categories like "most likely to cry at the ceremony".
7. **Photo booth with props:** caps, diplomas and signs with the school name and year.
8. **Teacher quotes.** Read quotes from the graduate's favorite teachers, and guests guess who said it.
9. **"How well do you know the grad".** Ten questions, answered on paper or from phones. Our [how well do you know me questions](/blog/how-well-do-you-know-me-questions/) are a good place to start.

## What are good college graduation party games?

College graduation party games can be a little more grown up: trivia about campus life, a "first job" prediction game and a toast round. Friends from different years of college get to meet through the game.

10. **Campus trivia.** Questions about the graduate's dorm, major, favorite dining hall meal and weirdest class.
11. **Two truths and a lie, college edition.** The graduate shares three stories from college, and guests vote on the lie.
12. **First job bingo.** Bingo cards with things like "moves back home" or "gets a dog", checked off over the next year.
13. **Toast round.** Five friends, one minute each.
14. **Name the major.** Guests guess the major from three clues about friends' classes.

## What are outdoor graduation party games?

Outdoor graduation party games are classic lawn games: cornhole, giant Jenga, ladder toss and a water balloon toss. Set them up away from the food, and let guests play in teams that mix families and friends.

15. **Cornhole** with boards painted in the school colors.
16. **Giant Jenga,** with a question about the graduate written on some blocks.
17. **Ladder toss.**
18. **Water balloon toss** for a hot afternoon.
19. **Diploma relay.** Teams pass a rolled-up "diploma" down the line without using their hands.

## What are indoor graduation party games?

Indoor graduation party games work in a living room or a rented hall: trivia on a TV, a memory jar, a guessing game and charades about school life. They also save the party if it rains.

20. **Graduation charades:** "losing your locker key", "pulling an all-nighter", "the cafeteria line".
21. **Memory jar.** Each guest writes one memory of the graduate, and the jar is opened at dinner.
22. **Guess the baby photo** with photos of the graduate and their friends.
23. **Trivia on the TV.** General questions for warm-up, then questions about the graduate. For ready questions, see our [trivia questions for adults](/blog/general-knowledge-trivia-questions/).

## Which graduation party games work for large groups and family?

For large groups and families, choose games that everyone can play at once without rules to explain: trivia on a big screen, a guessing game with photos or a memory jar. Mix teams of grandparents, cousins and friends.

24. **Family team trivia.** Each table is a team, and each team has at least one grandparent and one friend from school.
25. **The longest distance award.** Guests line up by how far they traveled, and the winner gets the first slice of cake.

> **Example scenario (not a real event):** A backyard party for a high school graduate with 70 guests. In "Guess the year", photo 9 shows her in a soccer uniform three sizes too big. Her friends guess freshman year, her grandmother says fourth grade, and her grandmother is right. She also tells the story of the uniform, which nobody else knew.

## What can you do instead of a graduation party?

Instead of a party, you can plan a family dinner, a trip with close friends, a joint party with other graduates or a small celebration with one shared activity. A joint party works well when friends graduate together.

[Emily Post's advice on graduation parties](https://emilypost.com/advice/hosting-a-graduation-party) describes two formats: a party with a set start time, or an open house that guests drop into. Other options:

- **A family dinner** at the graduate's favorite restaurant.
- **A trip** with a parent or a few friends.
- **A joint party** with two or three other graduates and their families.
- **A movie or game night** with close friends.

## How can guests share memories of the graduate?

Ask guests for stories and photos before the party, then share them in a slideshow, a memory book or a game in which guests guess which story belongs to which year. Friends who can't come can send a video.
