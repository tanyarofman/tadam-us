Eight nights, the same family, the same latkes. Trivia is the easiest way to turn one more candle lighting into a night everyone talks about. The first part here is questions about the holiday, with answers. The second part is the really funny one: questions about your own family.

## When is Hanukkah 2026, and which night is best for trivia?

Hanukkah 2026 begins at sundown on Friday, December 4, and lasts eight nights. The best night for trivia is the one when the most family members are together, often the first Friday or the Saturday after.

[Hebcal's Hanukkah 2026 calendar](https://www.hebcal.com/holidays/chanukah-2026) puts the holiday from the 25th of Kislev to the 2nd of Tevet, which ends on Saturday, December 12. This year the first candle is lit on a Friday night, so the first night already comes with a family dinner. Play right after the candles and before dessert, while everyone's still at the table.

## How do you host Hanukkah trivia at home?

Choose a host, split into mixed-age teams, and alternate holiday questions with family questions. A visible score and a small prize turn it into a real competition.

1. **Choose a host.** One person reads the questions and keeps the pace. Kids aged 10 to 13 make great hosts.
2. **Make teams.** Grandparents with grandkids, cousins against parents.
3. **Mix the rounds.** A holiday question, a family question, a photo question, then repeat.
4. **Keep the score visible.** A sheet on the table, a whiteboard on the fridge, or a screen.
5. **Have a small prize.** The winning team lights the next candle, or gets first pick of the jelly doughnuts.

## Which Hanukkah trivia questions suit which age?

Young kids need short questions with one-word answers, while adults enjoy history and details nobody remembers. Use the table to choose.

| Audience | Question type | Example |
|---|---|---|

| Kids (ages 9 to 12) | The story and its people | Who was the Maccabee? |
| Teens and adults | History, sources, details | Is Hanukkah in the Hebrew Bible? |
| All ages together | Your own family | Who ate the most latkes last year? |

## What are easy Hanukkah trivia questions for kids?

Ask about what kids see and do on Hanukkah: the candles, the dreidel, the food and the gelt. Any child from first grade can answer these ten.

1. **How many nights does Hanukkah last?** Eight.
2. **What's the candle that lights all the others called?** The shamash, or helper candle.
3. **Which four letters are on a dreidel outside Israel?** Nun, gimel, hei and shin.
4. **What do the dreidel letters stand for?** "Nes gadol haya sham," a great miracle happened there.
5. **Why do we eat foods fried in oil on Hanukkah?** To remember the oil that lasted eight days.
6. **What are potato pancakes fried for Hanukkah called?** Latkes.
7. **What do you call the chocolate coins given on Hanukkah?** Gelt.
8. **What's another name for Hanukkah?** The Festival of Lights.
9. **How many candles burn on the fifth night, counting the shamash?** Six.
10. **What are the jelly doughnuts eaten on Hanukkah called in Hebrew?** Sufganiyot.

## What are Hanukkah trivia questions for teens and adults?

Ask about the story itself: who the Maccabees were, where they came from and what happened at the Temple. These suit older kids and anyone who remembers a little from Hebrew school.

11. **On which Hebrew date does Hanukkah begin?** The 25th of Kislev.
12. **What does the word "Hanukkah" mean?** Dedication, for the rededication of the Temple in Jerusalem.
13. **Which town did the Maccabee family come from?** Modi'in.
14. **What was the name of the father who started the revolt?** Mattathias.
15. **How many sons did Mattathias have?** Five: John, Simon, Judah, Eleazar and Jonathan.
16. **Which son was called "the Maccabee"?** Judah.
17. **Which Greek king's decrees led to the revolt?** Antiochus IV, called Epiphanes.
18. **How long should the jar of oil have lasted?** One day. It lasted eight.
19. **How many candles do you light over all eight nights, not counting the shamash?** 36.
20. **And counting the shamash?** 44.

## What are hard Hanukkah trivia questions?

Ask about the sources, the debates and a few American firsts. These are the questions that get Grandpa arguing with the grandkid who just learned it this week.

21. **Is the Hanukkah story in the Hebrew Bible?** No. The revolt is told in the Books of Maccabees, and the miracle of the oil first appears in the Talmud, [in tractate Shabbat 21b](https://www.sefaria.org/Shabbat.21b), which asks "What is Hanukkah."
22. **Which Maccabee brother was killed when a war elephant fell on him?** Eleazar, as told in [1 Maccabees, chapter 6](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/1maccabees/6).
23. **Which school of sages said to light eight candles on the first night and one fewer each night?** The House of Shammai. The House of Hillel said to start with one and add one each night, and that's the custom followed today.
24. **Which heroine is linked to Hanukkah, and why do some families eat cheese on the holiday?** Judith. In her story, she fed an enemy general salty cheese and wine before defeating him.
25. **How many branches did the menorah in the Temple have?** Seven. A Hanukkah menorah holds eight candles plus the shamash.
26. **Which prayer is added to the daily prayers and grace after meals on Hanukkah?** Al HaNissim, "for the miracles."
27. **Which candle do you light first each night?** The newest one, on the left. You add candles from right to left and light from left to right.
28. **In which year was a public menorah first lit near the White House?** 1979, in Lafayette Park. It later grew into the [National Menorah](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/why-theres-30-foot-menorah-national-mall-180961553/), lit every year near the White House.
29. **In which year did Thanksgiving and the first full day of Hanukkah fall on the same day, nicknamed "Thanksgivukkah"?** [2013](https://brandeis.edu/now/2013/November/thanksgivukkah.html), and it won't happen again for tens of thousands of years.
30. **Which old spinning top game is the dreidel based on?** The teetotum, a betting top played in England and Ireland, according to the [Library of Congress Folklife Today blog](https://blogs.loc.gov/folklife/2022/12/the-truth-behind-the-hanukkah-dreidel-metafolklore-play-and-spin/).

## How do you write funny Hanukkah trivia questions about your own family?

Ask every family member in advance for one question about something only they know. Every question becomes a little story, and everyone else guesses.

This is where your trivia stops being "one more Hanukkah quiz from the internet" and becomes something that only happens at your house. Here are ten questions to copy and fill in:

31. Who in the family ate the most latkes last Hanukkah?
32. Which grandkid once set a napkin on fire during candle lighting?
33. What does Grandma always say when the latkes come out of the pan?
34. Which cousin holds the record for the longest dreidel spin?
35. Which year did the whole family get stuck in traffic on the way to a Hanukkah party, and where were you going?
36. Who sings the Hanukkah blessings with the most wrong words?
37. What was the strangest Hanukkah gift anyone here ever got?
38. Who's team applesauce and who's team sour cream?
39. Whose childhood photo is this? (Show a photo of a relative at age 5 and let everyone guess.)
40. What did Grandpa do on Hanukkah when he was the grandkids' age?

> Tip: collect the family questions in the family group chat a week ahead. That way everyone arrives with an answer only they know.
