"How well do you know me?" is the quiz that turns a group of guests into a competition about one person. It works with two best friends on a couch, with a family at a holiday table and with 80 guests at a 40th birthday. Here are 100 questions in five sets, and how to run the quiz so it ends with a clear winner.

## What is a "how well do you know me" quiz?

It's a quiz about one person: their tastes, habits and stories. Friends or family answer, and whoever gets the most answers right knows them best.

The quiz has three common forms. One person makes a quiz about themselves and sends it to friends. A couple tests each other, like in the [newlywed game](/blog/newlywed-game-questions/). Or, at a party, the organizer builds a quiz about the guest of honor and every guest plays. The questions are the same in all three; only the person asking changes.

## How do you make a how well do you know me quiz?

Choose 15 to 20 questions with one clear answer, write the answers down before anyone plays, and decide how you'll reveal them. Mix easy questions with a few that only close friends will get.

1. **Pick the person.** Yourself, a friend, a partner or the guest of honor at a party.
2. **Pick the questions.** Start from the lists below. About two thirds should be easy, so everyone scores some points.
3. **Collect the answers.** If the quiz is about someone else, ask them directly, or ask a person who knows them well.
4. **Turn some into multiple choice.** "What's her favorite pizza topping?" is hard as an open question and fun with four options.
5. **Choose a format.** Paper, a group chat, slides on a TV or a live game on phones. The table below compares them.

| | Paper | Group chat | Slides on a TV | Live game on phones |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Setup** | Print answer sheets | Type the questions in the chat | Build slides with the answers | Questions and answers loaded into a game |
| **Scoring** | Swap sheets and mark | The person checks each reply | Players write answers, the host counts | Automatic, with live scores |
| **Best for** | A small table, 5 to 15 people | Friends in different cities | A living room, 10 to 30 people | A party of any size |

## What are how well do you know me questions for a birthday party?

Birthday questions mix the birthday person's past with the year that just passed: their best memory, their proudest moment, their plans for the next year. They work as a game for all the guests, with the birthday person revealing the answers.

Questions 91, 92 and 99 are the deep ones. They work well near the end, after the room has warmed up. Personal questions like these tend to bring people closer: in a well-known study, [strangers who asked each other increasingly personal questions felt closer](https://ggia.berkeley.edu/practice/36_questions_for_increasing_closeness) than strangers who only made small talk.

> **Example scenario (not a real event):** At a 40th birthday for 50 guests, question 62 asks for the name of the birthday girl's first dog. Her college roommates guess "Max". Her mom answers "Biscuit" without looking up, and her younger brother answers "Biscuit, the second one". He wins the round, and the story of Biscuit the first fills the next five minutes.

## How do you score the quiz and pick a winner?

Give one point for every right answer, and two points for the hard questions near the end. In a tie, ask one bonus question, and whoever answers first and right wins.

On paper, someone has to collect the sheets, mark them and add up the scores, usually while the party waits. That's fine for a table of ten and slow for a room of 60.
