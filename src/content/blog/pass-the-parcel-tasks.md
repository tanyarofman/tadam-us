Some games need no explaining. A circle, music, a parcel going from hand to hand, and everyone hoping the music stops when it reaches them. What turns pass the parcel from one more party game into a moment people remember is what's written inside each layer. Here are 60 ready-made forfeits and questions, by age.

## What is pass the parcel, and how do you play?

Pass the parcel is a party game in which a prize wrapped in many layers goes round a circle while music plays. When the music stops, whoever is holding the parcel unwraps one layer, and the game carries on until the last layer is off.

It's a [classic British party game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pass_the_parcel), played at children's birthday parties and at Christmas. In the traditional version, a small prize waits in every layer. Many families now put a forfeit or a question in each layer instead, and save the main prize for whoever unwraps the last one.

## How do you wrap a pass the parcel?

Choose a prize, wrap it in one layer per player, and tuck a note into every layer. It takes about half an hour.

1. **Choose a main prize.** Something every player would be happy to win.
2. **Write the notes.** A forfeit or a question on each one. Mix easy ones with harder ones.
3. **Wrap from the inside out.** Layer, note, layer, note. Change the paper colour each time, so it's clear where a layer ends. Newspaper works fine for the inner layers.
4. **Go easy on the sellotape.** One piece per layer, or every turn takes a minute.
5. **Make a playlist.** Someone with their back to the circle stops the music, so there are no complaints.

## How many layers do you need, and what goes inside?

One layer per player plus two or three spare, and one prize in the middle. The table shows what suits each age.

| Age | Layers | On the notes | Inside |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 to 6 | 8 to 12 | Moves and sounds, no reading | A sweet in every layer, a prize in the middle |
| 7 to 11 | 12 to 20 | Short forfeits and questions | A prize in the middle, maybe two surprises |
| Teens | 12 to 20 | Questions and "pass it to" notes | One prize everyone wants |
| Adults | 10 to 15 | Funny forfeits, personal questions | A silly prize or a voucher |

## Which pass the parcel forfeits suit kids?

Short movement, sound and acting forfeits that take up to half a minute and don't embarrass anyone. You can copy these fifteen exactly as they are.

1. Hop on one foot ten times.
2. Make an animal noise, and let everyone guess the animal.
3. Sing a line of "Happy Birthday" in a robot voice.
4. Say "She sells seashells by the seashore" three times, fast.
5. Act out a job without words, until someone guesses it.
6. Waddle like a penguin round the circle.
7. Pull your funniest face for ten seconds.
8. Count down from ten to one in a whisper.
9. High five everyone in the circle.
10. Dance for ten seconds with no music.
11. Say something nice to the person on your right.
12. Act out a sport without words, until someone guesses it.
13. Stand like a statue until someone makes you laugh.
14. Spin round three times, then walk in a straight line.
15. Make up a short dance move, and everyone copies you.

## What questions can you put in pass the parcel?

Short questions with no wrong answer, the kind every child can answer and that reveal something about them. They suit ages seven to eleven, and teenagers too.

16. If you could only eat one food for the rest of your life, what would it be?
17. Which superpower would you choose?
18. What's the funniest thing that happened to you this year?
19. Where would you fly to tomorrow morning?
20. Which animal is most like you, and why?
21. What would you do with a million pounds?
22. Which school subject would you scrap, and which would you add?
23. Which song do you sing in the shower?
24. What's the first thing you do when you get home from school?
25. What's the best present you've ever had?
26. What are you best at?
27. Which cartoon character would you most like to meet?
28. What would you change if you were the teacher for one day?
29. What's your dream for the summer holidays?
30. Which food can you least stand?

## Which "pass it to" notes suit teenagers?

Notes that send the parcel to someone else in the group, for something nice or funny. That way, even someone the music keeps skipping gets a turn.

Stick to kind notes. "Pass it to the most annoying person here" sounds funny when you write it, and much less so when it's read out.

31. Pass it to whoever always makes you laugh.
32. Pass it to whoever is best at keeping a secret.
33. Pass it to whoever is always last to arrive.
34. Pass it to whoever you'd take to a desert island.
35. Pass it to whoever has the most contagious laugh.
36. Pass it to whoever always knows what the homework is.
37. Pass it to whoever has the best taste in music.
38. Pass it to whoever will be famous one day.
39. Pass it to whoever you met most recently.
40. Pass it to whoever is wearing the most colours today.

## What are funny pass the parcel forfeits for adults?

Forfeits that involve the rest of the room, that are funny without humiliating anyone, and that you can do without getting up. They work at milestone birthdays, Christmas parties, hen dos and evenings with friends.

41. Tell a terrible joke, and don't be the first to laugh.
42. Do an impression of someone in the room until people guess who.
43. Sing the chorus of the first song that comes into your head.
44. Raise a short toast to the person on your left.
45. Tell everyone your most embarrassing moment of the past year.
46. Show everyone the last photo on your phone (one skip allowed).
47. Speak in an accent of your choice until your next turn.
48. Give a 30-second speech on "why I'm the best parker in this room."
49. Act out a film without words until someone guesses it.
50. Text "I love you" to a family member right now, and read out the reply.
51. Share something nobody in the room knows about you.
52. Pick someone in the room and invent an election slogan for them.

## How do you make a personal pass the parcel about the guest of honour?

Swap some of the notes for questions about the person you're celebrating. Whoever gets it right stays safe, and whoever gets it wrong does a forfeit.

This turns pass the parcel from a general party game into a game about the birthday girl, the bride-to-be or a retiring colleague. Ask family and close friends for the answers in advance, so someone can settle arguments. Here are eight to start with, and our [how well do you know me questions](/blog/how-well-do-you-know-me-questions/) have plenty more:

53. What do they always order at a restaurant?
54. Which phrase do they say all the time?
55. What are they most afraid of?
56. What was their childhood nickname?
57. Which song gets them on the dance floor in a second?
58. Which hobby did they start and give up after a week?
59. Where was their most memorable holiday?
60. What did they want to be when they grew up?

## How do you play pass the parcel with a lot of guests?

Up to 20 players, one circle is enough. Beyond that, split into several circles, or switch to a game where everyone answers every question.
