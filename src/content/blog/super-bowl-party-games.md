Half the room at a Super Bowl party is watching every play, and the other half is there for the food, the commercials and each other. Good party games work for both. Here are 20 games for every stage of the night, a football trivia round about the rules, and no-money bets that keep everyone invested until the last whistle.

## What are fun games for a Super Bowl party?

Choose one game for each part of the night: predictions before kickoff, squares or bingo during the quarters, and trivia at halftime. Make every game playable for people who don't follow football.

The 2027 game is on [Sunday, February 14, 2027](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_LXI), which is also Valentine's Day. Expect a few couples to arrive with flowers, and plan at least one game they can play as a pair.

Here's what to play at each stage:

| Stage | Game | Players | Supplies |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before kickoff | 1. Prediction sheet | Everyone | Printed sheets, pens |
| Before kickoff | 2. Guess the coin toss | Everyone | Nothing |
| Before kickoff | 3. Snack draft | Teams of 2 to 4 | The food on the table |
| During the quarters | 4. Football squares | Up to 100 squares | A 10 by 10 grid |
| During the quarters | 5. Commercial bingo | Everyone | Bingo cards |
| During the quarters | 6. Rate the commercials | Everyone | Scorecards |
| During the quarters | 7. Touchdown dance-off | Anyone brave | Music |
| During the quarters | 8. Announcer phrase bingo | Everyone | Bingo cards |
| Halftime | 9. Football trivia | Teams of 3 to 6 | Questions, answer sheets |
| Halftime | 10. Halftime show bingo | Everyone | Bingo cards |
| Halftime | 11. Cornhole tournament | Pairs | Cornhole boards |
| Halftime | 12. Paper football | Pairs | Folded paper, a table |
| Halftime | 13. Who knows the host best | Everyone | 10 questions |
| Any time | 14. Backyard catch challenge | Kids and adults | A football |
| Any time | 15. Football toss target | Kids | A football, a hula hoop |
| Any time | 16. Design your own jersey | Kids | Paper, markers |
| Any time | 17. Guess how many wings | Everyone | A jar or a tally |
| After the game | 18. Final score winner | Everyone | The prediction sheets |
| After the game | 19. Party MVP vote | Everyone | Slips of paper |
| After the game | 20. Party awards | Everyone | Homemade certificates |

**1. Prediction sheet.** Before kickoff, every guest fills in a sheet: the winner, the final score, the first team to score, the total number of touchdowns. Check it after the game and crown the best guesser.

**4. Football squares.** Draw a 10 by 10 grid, guests write their names in squares, and then you draw random numbers 0 to 9 for each row and column, one for each team. At the end of each quarter, the square that matches the last digit of each team's score wins. Play for prizes, not money.

**13. Who knows the host best.** Ten questions about the host or the birthday person in the room, answered at halftime. It works even better if you build it from [how well do you know me questions](/blog/how-well-do-you-know-me-questions/).

## What are Super Bowl party games for people who don't watch football?

Give non-fans games about the commercials, the halftime show and the food. Commercial bingo, rating the commercials, a snack draft and party awards need no football knowledge at all.

- **Commercial bingo (5).** Each square is something that shows up in big-game commercials: a talking animal, a baby, a car on a mountain road, a famous face (don't name anyone on the card), a sequel to last year's commercial.
- **Rate the commercials (6).** Score every commercial from 1 to 10 for funny, heartwarming and confusing. At the end, the group crowns the best one.
- **Halftime show bingo (10).** Squares like costume change, fireworks, a guest performer, a slow song, the crowd singing along. Keep the card about the show, not about any named performer.
- **Snack draft (3).** Teams take turns picking dishes from the table. After the game, everyone votes for the best plate.
- **Party awards (20).** Loudest fan, best snack, best outfit, most surprising prediction.

## What are Super Bowl party games for kids and families?

Get kids moving between plays: a backyard catch challenge, a football toss through a hula hoop, or designing their own jersey. Keep a quiet table with paper football and coloring for younger kids.

- **Backyard catch challenge (14).** Count how many catches in a row each pair can make during a commercial break.
- **Football toss target (15).** Hang a hula hoop and give points for every throw through it, with a closer line for the youngest.
- **Design your own jersey (16).** Kids invent a team name, colors and a number, and wear their paper jersey for the second half.
- **Paper football (12).** A folded paper triangle, flicked across a table between two fingers held up as goal posts.
- **Guess how many wings (17).** Kids keep the tally on a whiteboard, and the closest guess wins.

## What are good football trivia questions for a party?

Ask about the rules and the history of the game, not about specific teams or players. That way fans can show off and newcomers learn something, and the questions stay right year after year.

The scoring answers come from Britannica's entries on the [touchdown](https://www.britannica.com/sports/touchdown), the [field goal](https://www.britannica.com/sports/field-goal) and the [safety](https://www.britannica.com/sports/safety-football-score).

1. How many points is a touchdown worth? **6**
2. How many points is a field goal worth? **3**
3. How many points is a safety worth? **2**
4. How many points is the kick after a touchdown worth? **1**
5. How many points is a two-point conversion worth? **2**
6. In which year did a touchdown become worth six points? **1912**
7. In which year did a field goal become worth three points? **1909**
8. How many players does each team have on the field at once? **11**
9. How long is the field between the goal lines? **100 yards**
10. How deep is each end zone? **10 yards**
11. How many quarters are in a game? **4**
12. How many downs does the offense get to gain 10 yards? **4**
13. How many yards does the offense need for a first down? **10**
14. What's it called when a defender catches a pass meant for the other team? **An interception**
15. What's it called when the quarterback is tackled behind the line of scrimmage? **A sack**
16. What's it called when a player drops the ball while carrying it? **A fumble**
17. What's the Roman numeral of the 2027 big game? **LXI (61)**
18. On which holiday does the 2027 game fall? **Valentine's Day**
19. In which state is the 2027 game played? **California**
20. In which month and year was the first big game played? **January 1967**

## What are fun no-money bets for a Super Bowl party?

Bet on predictions with stakes that cost nothing: the loser does the dishes, wears a silly hat or gives up the last slice. Keep it to bragging rights, so nobody leaves the party owing anyone.

Things to predict, with a point for each right call:

- Heads or tails on the coin toss.
- Which team scores first, and how: a touchdown, a field goal or a safety.
- Whether the first score comes in the first five minutes.
- How many commercials have an animal in them.
- Whether the halftime show includes a costume change.
- Whether any score ends in a 7.
- The final score, closest guess wins.

Stakes that work: dish duty, carrying the trash out, wearing a foam finger for the second half, or letting the winner pick next year's menu.

## When do you play the games: kickoff, halftime or commercials?

Start predictions and the coin toss before kickoff, keep squares and bingo running through the quarters, and save the bigger games for halftime. Announce the winners right after the final whistle, while everyone is still there.

- **Before kickoff:** prediction sheets, the coin toss, the snack draft. Hand out bingo cards.
- **During the quarters:** squares and bingo run on their own. Commercial breaks are for quick kids' games.
- **Halftime:** the longest break, good for trivia, cornhole or a game about your guests.
- **After the game:** check the prediction sheets, hand out party awards, and let the winners choose the leftovers.
