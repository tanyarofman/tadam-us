Sixteen is a turning point: a learner's permit or a driver's license, a first job, and friends who matter more than ever. A sweet 16 party can be as big as a small wedding or as small as five friends and a movie. Here are 35 sweet 16 party ideas, at home, out and for small groups, plus themes and games.

## How do you plan a sweet 16 party?

Start with the birthday teen: ask whether they want a big party, a small group or a day out. Then set the guest list, the place and the date, and send invitations four to six weeks ahead.

1. **Ask what they want,** and listen. A sweet 16 is the teen's party, not the parents'.
2. **Set the guest list together:** school friends, team friends, cousins and family.
3. **Choose a place:** at home, a rented space or an outing.
4. **Pick a theme** and one or two activities.
5. **Agree on house rules** before the party, like who can come and when it ends.

## What are good sweet 16 party ideas at home?

At home, a sweet 16 works as a backyard party, a movie night, a karaoke party or a spa night. Add lights, a photo corner and good food, and the house feels like a venue.

6. **Backyard party** with string lights, music and a dessert table.
7. **Outdoor movie night** with a projector, blankets and popcorn.
8. **Karaoke party** with a playlist of their favorite songs.
9. **Spa night** with face masks, nail polish and a chocolate fountain.
10. **Pizza and game night** with board games and video games.
11. **Taco or burger bar** where guests build their own.
12. **Photo corner** with a backdrop, props and an instant camera.
13. **Bonfire night** with s'mores, if there's space.

## What are sweet 16 party ideas for small groups?

For a small group, choose something you can only do with a few friends: a sleepover, a cooking class, a day trip or a fancy dinner. Small parties feel special when the plan is personal.

14. **Sleepover** with a movie marathon and breakfast in the morning.
15. **Cooking or baking class** for five or six friends.
16. **Fancy dinner** at a nice restaurant, with everyone dressed up.
17. **Day trip** to a city, a beach or an amusement park.
18. **Pottery or painting class.**
19. **Picnic in a park** with a playlist and lawn games.

## Where can you have a sweet 16 party?

Popular places for a sweet 16 are rented halls, restaurants with a private room, bowling alleys, escape rooms, trampoline parks and rooftop or garden venues. Choose by the size of the group and how much you want to plan yourself.

20. **Escape room** for a group of six to ten.
21. **Bowling alley** with a private lane area.
22. **Trampoline park** or a climbing gym.
23. **Restaurant with a private room.**
24. **Rented hall** for a big party with a DJ.
25. **Mini golf** followed by ice cream.

## What are good themes for a sweet 16?

Good sweet 16 themes are glamour nights, decades, neon glow parties, garden parties and candy themes. Let the theme guide the decor, the dress code and one activity, and keep the rest simple.

Pink and gold are popular sweet 16 colors, but the best theme is the one that fits the teen.

| Theme | Decor | Activity | Dress code |
|---|---|---|---|
| Glamour night | Gold, black | Photo shoot | Formal |
| Neon glow | Black lights | Glow dance | Neon, white |
| Candy land | Pastel, sweets | Candy bar | Bright |
| Garden party | Flowers, lights | Flower crowns | Sundress |
| 2000s throwback | Flip phones | Karaoke | Y2K |
| Masquerade | Masks, candles | Mystery game | Formal |

26. **Candy theme** with a candy bar and sweets in jars.
27. **Glow party** with glow sticks and black lights.
28. **Masquerade** with masks at the door.

## What activities and games can you do at a sweet 16 party?

The best sweet 16 activities are a quiz about the birthday teen, a photo booth, karaoke and a dance-off. Games about the birthday teen work especially well, because friends and family get to show what they know.

29. **"How well do you know" quiz** about the birthday teen. Our [how well do you know me questions](/blog/how-well-do-you-know-me-questions/) have 100 questions to start from.
30. **Dance-off** with songs from different decades.
31. **Guess the baby photo** with photos of the birthday teen and friends.
32. **Sixteen candles:** 16 people each light a candle and share a memory or a wish.

> **Example scenario (not a real event):** A sweet 16 in a rented hall with 40 friends and family. In the quiz, question 7 asks what the birthday girl wanted to be at age six. Her friends guess a singer. Her grandfather says a garbage truck driver, because she waved at the truck every Tuesday. He's right, and the whole room cheers.

## What are sweet 16 party ideas for boys?

For boys, a 16th birthday often centers on an activity: a game night, a sports day, paintball, go-karts or a trip with friends. Most of the ideas above work for any teen, so plan around what he enjoys.

33. **Go-kart or paintball day** with a few friends.
34. **Video game tournament** at home with a bracket on the wall.

## What are sweet 16 ideas instead of a party?

Instead of a party, many teens choose a trip, a concert, a special dinner with family or one big experience. Some families pair a small gathering with a gift that marks the age, like driving lessons.

35. **A trip** with a parent or a best friend.

Other ideas: a concert, a hot air balloon ride, a weekend in a city they've wanted to visit, or a family dinner where each relative brings a letter.

Whatever the plan, the American Academy of Pediatrics advises that [a parent should be at home during a teen party](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/teen/Pages/A-Parents-Guide-To-Teen-Parties.aspx), with rules agreed on beforehand. You can stay out of the way and still be there. For a party with family and friends of all ages, see also our [graduation party games](/blog/graduation-party-games/), which work for a 16th too.

Make it personal: collect messages, photos and stories from friends and family, and give the birthday teen a moment to hear them. Inside jokes and old photos are what guests remember.
