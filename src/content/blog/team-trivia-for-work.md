## What is team trivia, and how does it work?

Team trivia is a quiz in which players answer as teams, not as individuals. A quizmaster reads the questions, each team agrees on one answer, and the team with the most points at the end wins.

At work, teams make trivia friendlier than a solo quiz. Nobody is put on the spot, people who don't usually work together have to talk, and a team can win even if no one in it knows the most facts. It's one of the [team building activities for work](/blog/team-building-activities-for-work/) that needs almost no budget or preparation.

## How do you host team trivia at work?

Pick a quizmaster, split people into teams of four to six, and play three or four rounds of 10 questions. Give one point per correct answer, show the scores between rounds and keep a tie-breaker ready.

1. **Choose a quizmaster** who won't play. They read the questions, give the time limit and settle arguments.
2. **Make mixed teams.** Mix departments, new hires and long-timers. Let each team choose a name.
3. **Hand out answer sheets** or use a simple form on phones, one per team.
4. **Set a time limit,** about 30 seconds per question, and read each question twice.
5. **Collect answers after each round** and read the correct ones aloud. Teams love to argue about the close calls.
6. **Show the scores between rounds** on a whiteboard or a shared screen.
7. **Keep a tie-breaker:** a number question like "How many minutes are in a week", where the closest answer wins (10,080).

| 1 | Easy warm-up | 10 | 8 |
| 2 | Funny facts | 10 | 8 |
| 3 | Multiple choice | 10 | 8 |
| 4 | About the team | 10 | 12 |

## What are good multiple choice trivia questions for work meetings?

Multiple choice questions are fast, because teams pick a letter instead of writing an answer. They fit well at the start of a meeting or in a chat window, with three options and one clear answer.

38. What does "CC" stand for in an email? A) Carbon copy B) Copy content C) Clear copy. **A**
39. Which company invented the sticky note? A) IBM B) 3M C) Xerox. **B**
40. In which year was the first network email sent? A) 1965 B) 1971 C) 1983. **B**
41. What is the largest desert in the world? A) Sahara B) Gobi C) Antarctica. **C**
42. How many time zones does the contiguous United States have? A) Three B) Four C) Five. **B**
43. Which element has the symbol Fe? A) Iron B) Lead C) Tin. **A**
44. How many letters are in the top row of a standard keyboard? A) 8 B) 10 C) 12. **B**
45. What is the smallest US state by area? A) Delaware B) Connecticut C) Rhode Island. **C**
46. What does "www" stand for in a web address? A) World Wide Web B) Web World Wide C) Wide World Web. **A**
47. Which symbol did the inventor of network email choose to separate the user from the computer? A) # B) @ C) &. **B**

The first network email was [sent in 1971 by Ray Tomlinson](https://lemelson.mit.edu/resources/ray-tomlinson), who also picked the @ sign. That makes question 47 a good one for a team of engineers.

## What are good trivia questions about your coworkers?

The best work trivia questions are about the team itself: who has lived where, who did what before this job, whose desk plant is the oldest. Collect the answers with a short anonymous form a week before the game.

48. Who on the team has lived in the most states?
49. Whose first job was at a fast food restaurant?
50. Who has the longest commute?
51. Which teammate has been to the most countries?
52. Whose pet is in the most video call backgrounds?
53. Who sent the very first message in the team chat?
54. Who was a competitive athlete in high school?
55. Who can speak the most languages?
56. Whose childhood dream job was the furthest from what they do now?
57. Which team photo is from the oldest company event?

Only ask what people agreed to share, and leave out anything about health, family problems or salary. For gentler warm-up questions, see our [icebreaker questions for work](/blog/icebreaker-questions/).

> **Example scenario (not a real event):** A sales team of 24 plays four rounds after the quarterly meeting. "The Mute Button" leads after the funny facts round, until question 56: whose childhood dream job was the furthest from sales. Nobody guesses that the quiet accountant wanted to be a rodeo clown. Her team wins by one point, and the trophy moves to her desk.

## Can you do trivia on Microsoft Teams or Zoom?

Yes. Share your screen with the questions, let teams answer in the chat or a form, and use breakout rooms so each team can talk privately before answering.

In Microsoft Teams, the quizmaster [presents slides or a window from the Share button](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/present-content-in-microsoft-teams-meetings-fcc2bf59-aecd-4481-8f99-ce55dd836ce8). In Zoom, the host can [split a meeting into up to 100 breakout rooms](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0062540), one per team, and bring everyone back for the answers. Keep rounds a little shorter online, and send each team's answers to the quizmaster in a private message so other teams can't copy.
