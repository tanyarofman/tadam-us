Thanksgiving gives a family a whole day together: hours before the turkey is ready, a long meal, and the slow stretch before pie. A few good games fill those hours without anyone reaching for their phone. Here are 25 Thanksgiving family games for every age and every part of the day, plus 12 trivia questions about the holiday.

## What are Thanksgiving family games for all ages?

Games for all ages need simple rules and mixed teams: charades with Thanksgiving words, a "this or that" round about pies and sides, and guessing baby photos of the people at the table. A 6 year old and a grandparent can play all three side by side.

- **8. Charades.** Write words on slips of paper: carving the turkey, watching the parade, the last slice of pie, falling asleep on the couch. Act them out without words.
- **9. This or that: pies.** Pumpkin or pecan, stuffing or mashed potatoes, dark meat or white. Everyone guesses how the rest of the family will vote before the hands go up.
- **10. Baby photo guess.** Ask everyone to send a baby photo ahead of time, number them on a board and guess who's who.
- **13. Family survey game.** Before the day, ask everyone the same five questions, like "the best part of Thanksgiving". At the party, two teams guess the most common answers.
- **22. Cornhole.** A tournament in pairs, one younger and one older player, while the turkey rests.

## How do you keep kids entertained on Thanksgiving?

Give kids active games before dinner and a quiet activity at the table: a paper feather hunt, pin the tail on the turkey, a pumpkin roll relay and a kids' table sheet with puzzles and drawing. Short games and small prizes keep them busy until the turkey is carved.

- **4. Feather hunt.** Hide paper feathers around the house. Each color is worth different points.
- **5. Pin the tail on the turkey.** Draw a big turkey and give kids paper feathers to pin on blindfolded.
- **6. Pumpkin roll relay.** Kids roll a mini pumpkin across the yard with a wooden spoon, then hand it to the next player.
- **7. Kids' table sheet.** A sheet of paper on the kids' table with a word search, a maze and a turkey to color.
- **24. Paper plate turkeys.** Trace hands on colored paper for feathers and glue them on a paper plate. The best one goes on the front door.
- **3. Thankful tree.** Kids write or draw what they're thankful for on paper leaves and hang them on a branch in a vase.

## What are fun Thanksgiving games for adults?

Adults like games with a bit of competition and a lot of laughing: guess who cooked each dish, a blind pie taste test, two truths and a lie, and the family survey game. They work best between dinner and dessert.

- **11. Guess the cook.** Number every dish on the table without names. Everyone guesses who made what, and the cooks reveal themselves before dessert.
- **12. Blind pie taste test.** Blindfold the players and give them a bite of each pie. Points for naming the pie, and bonus points for naming who baked it.
- **16. Two truths and a lie.** Everyone shares three things that happened to them this year, and the table guesses the lie.
- **20. Gold turkey trophy.** Spray paint a toy turkey gold and give it to the family member who wins the most games of the day. It comes back every year.

## What games can you play while eating dinner?

At the table, play games that need only talking: the gratitude ABC, a question jar, two truths and a lie and guessing who wrote each thankful note. Everyone keeps eating, and the conversation goes beyond the weather.

- **14. Gratitude ABC.** Go around the table, and each person names something they're thankful for that starts with the next letter of the alphabet.
- **15. Question jar.** Fill a jar with questions like "the best thing that happened to you this year" or "a family recipe you want to learn". Pass it around during the meal.
- **1. Thankful jar guess.** When guests arrive, everyone writes something they're thankful for on a slip of paper, without a name. At dinner, read the slips and guess who wrote each one.

## What are some fun gratitude games to play on Thanksgiving?

Gratitude games turn "what are you thankful for" into play: guess who wrote each thankful note, toss a ball and answer the question under your thumb, or hang thankful notes on a tree. They work for every age and give quiet family members a turn.

- **1. Thankful jar guess.** The most popular one at the table, because the guessing makes the answers fun to hear.
- **2. Gratitude ball toss.** Write prompts on a beach ball: a person, a place, a food, a memory. Whoever catches it answers the prompt under their right thumb.
- **3. Thankful tree.** Keep the branch from year to year, and read last year's leaves before writing new ones.
- **14. Gratitude ABC.** Gets harder and funnier toward the end of the alphabet.

> **Example scenario (not a real event):** A family of 16 across three generations. In the thankful jar guess, nobody can figure out who wrote "the new dishwasher", until the grandfather admits it was him. After dinner, the 10 year old twins win the gold turkey trophy in the pie taste test.

## What are Thanksgiving trivia questions for family?

Ask about the history of the holiday and the food on the table: how long the 1621 feast lasted, when Thanksgiving became the fourth Thursday of November, and what a male turkey is called. Play in mixed teams between dinner and dessert.

The 1621 harvest feast at Plymouth lasted three days, as Edward Winslow wrote in a letter that [Plimoth Patuxet Museums](https://plimoth.org/for-students/homework-help/thanksgiving) quotes, and the Wampanoag guests brought five deer, according to the [National Museum of the American Indian](https://americanindian.si.edu/nk360/thanksgiving/sq4ss1.html). The National Archives keeps [Lincoln's 1863 proclamation](https://visit.archives.gov/whats-on/explore-exhibits/president-abraham-lincolns-thanksgiving-day-proclamation), written after years of letters from [Sarah Josepha Hale](https://www.nps.gov/liho/learn/historyculture/lincoln-and-thanksgiving.htm), and tells how Congress [fixed the fourth Thursday in 1941](https://www.archives.gov/legislative/features/thanksgiving), starting in 1942.

1. How many days did the 1621 harvest feast at Plymouth last? **Three**
2. About how many Wampanoag men came to the feast with their leader Massasoit? **About 90**
3. What did the Wampanoag bring to the feast? **Five deer**
4. In which year did Abraham Lincoln proclaim a national Thanksgiving? **1863**
5. Which writer campaigned for years for a national Thanksgiving and wrote to Lincoln in 1863? **Sarah Josepha Hale**
6. Since 1942, Thanksgiving falls on which Thursday of November? **The fourth**
7. In which year did the official White House turkey pardon begin? **1989**
8. When is Thanksgiving in Canada? **The second Monday in October**
9. What is a male turkey called? **A tom or a gobbler**
10. Can wild turkeys fly? **Yes, for short distances**
11. Cranberries, blueberries and which other fruit are native to North America? **Grapes (the American grape)**
12. Which US state grows the most pumpkins? **Illinois**

The answers for questions 7 to 12 come from the [White House Historical Association](https://whitehousehistory.org/pardoning-the-thanksgiving-turkey), [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Thanksgiving-Day), the [Smithsonian's National Zoo](https://nationalzoo.si.edu/animals/wild-turkey), the [University of Missouri](https://ipm.missouri.edu/MEG/2012/11/Cranberry-A-Holiday-Staple) and the [USDA](https://www.ers.usda.gov/newsroom/trending-topics/pumpkins-background-statistics). For a bonus question, Benjamin Franklin called the turkey "a much more respectable bird" than the bald eagle in a [1784 letter to his daughter](https://annotation.blogs.archives.gov/2020/11/25/talking-turkey) that he never sent.

## What are Thanksgiving family games with prizes?

The best Thanksgiving prizes come from the day itself: the wish from the wishbone, first pick of the leftovers, the last slice of pie or the gold turkey trophy. Skip money, and let the winner choose something the whole family will see.

- **18. Wishbone pull.** Save and dry the turkey's wishbone. Two players each hold one end and pull, and whoever ends up with the longer piece gets the wish.
- **19. Leftover picks.** Winners of each game pick their leftovers first, in order of points.
- **20. Gold turkey trophy.** The overall winner keeps it for a year and brings it back next Thanksgiving.
- **23. Family turkey trot.** A morning walk or run around the block before cooking starts. The winner chooses the first game of the day.
