A remote team can work together for years without knowing who has a dog, who grew up on a farm or who bakes bread on weekends. Those small facts are what make a team feel like people instead of square video tiles. Here are 25 virtual team building activities that fit into a video call, from 5-minute warm-ups to a full trivia game.

## What are virtual team building activities, and do they work?

Virtual team building activities are games, challenges and conversations that help remote coworkers get to know each other over video, chat or a shared screen. They work best when they are short, regular and optional.

Many teams need them. Gallup reports that [about half of remote-capable US employees work hybrid and about a quarter work fully remote](https://www.gallup.com/401384/indicator-hybrid-work.aspx), and in the overall workforce [22.6 percent of workers teleworked in March 2026](https://www.bls.gov/opub/ted/2026/22-6-percent-of-workers-teleworked-in-march-2026.htm), according to the Bureau of Labor Statistics. Gallup has also found that [fully remote employees report more loneliness](https://www.gallup.com/workplace/645566/remote-workers-face-higher-risks-loneliness.aspx) than people who work on-site: 25 percent compared with 16 percent.

## What is the 5-minute icebreaker activity?

A 5-minute icebreaker is a quick question or game at the start of a call, where everyone answers in one sentence or in the chat. It warms up the room without taking time from the agenda.

1. **One-word check-in.** Everyone describes their week in one word.
2. **Show something on your desk.** Each person holds up one object and explains it in a sentence.
3. **This or that.** Coffee or tea, mountains or beach, early bird or night owl. People answer in the chat.
4. **Rose and thorn.** One good thing and one hard thing from the week.
5. **Guess the view.** Everyone shares a photo of the view from their window, and the team guesses whose it is.

## What are fun virtual team building activities for Zoom?

The most fun Zoom activities use the screen and breakout rooms: trivia, a scavenger hunt at home or a guessing game with photos. Small groups in breakout rooms make quieter people more likely to speak.

6. **Team trivia in breakout rooms.** Each team discusses in its own room, then everyone returns for the answers. Our guide to [team trivia for work](/blog/team-trivia-for-work/) has a round plan and 40 questions.
7. **Home scavenger hunt.** The host calls out an item ("something older than you", "something blue"), and the first person to show it on camera wins the point.
8. **Baby photo guess.** People send baby photos ahead of time, and the team guesses who is who.
9. **Two truths and a lie.** Each person shares three facts, and the team votes on the lie.
10. **Virtual coffee roulette.** Random pairs meet in breakout rooms for 10 minutes with one question to start.
11. **Draw it in 60 seconds.** Use the Zoom whiteboard. One person draws a word, the rest guess.

The host can [split a Zoom meeting into up to 100 breakout rooms](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0062540), assigned automatically or by hand.

## Which virtual team building activities work in Microsoft Teams?

Any activity that runs on a shared screen and the chat works in Microsoft Teams: trivia, polls, show-and-tell and drawing games. Teams also has breakout rooms and a whiteboard, so most Zoom ideas carry over.

12. **Poll of the day.** A silly poll in the chat ("best pizza topping") with the results discussed at the end.
13. **Show-and-tell.** One person each week shares a hobby, a trip or a skill for five minutes.
14. **Emoji story.** The host posts a movie plot or a team project in emojis only, and the team decodes it.
15. **Guess the playlist.** Everyone sends one favorite song, and the team guesses who picked which.
16. **Team photo challenge.** A weekly theme ("your lunch", "your workspace"), with photos posted in a channel.
17. **Lightning talks.** Three people, three minutes each, on anything except work.

To run a game, the host [presents slides or a window from the Share button](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/present-content-in-microsoft-teams-meetings-fcc2bf59-aecd-4481-8f99-ce55dd836ce8), and players answer in the chat.

## What are free virtual team building activities?

Most good virtual activities are free: questions, show-and-tell, photo games and trivia only need the video tool your team already uses. The cost is preparation time, not money.

18. **Gratitude round.** Each person thanks one teammate for something specific.
19. **Online escape puzzle.** Teams solve a set of riddles you prepare in a shared document.
20. **Book or podcast club.** One pick a month, discussed for 30 minutes.
21. **Cook-along.** Everyone makes the same simple recipe on camera.
22. **Fitness break.** Ten minutes of stretching led by a different person each week.
23. **Desk tour.** One person a week shows their workspace and one object with a story.
24. **Wins wall.** A shared board where people post small wins, work or personal.
25. **Team quiz about the team.** Questions about each other, collected in a short form beforehand.

SHRM points out that [required social events often backfire](https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/news/why-most-virtual-team-building-fails-what-hr-can-do-about), and that optional formats like coffee chats and interest groups tend to work better. ## How do you choose an activity for a remote or hybrid team?

A few rules that keep it working:

- **Put it inside working hours.** An activity after hours feels like overtime.
- **Make it optional** and easy to join late or leave early.
- **Don't require cameras.** Pick activities that also work by voice or chat.
- **Rotate the host** so it isn't always the manager running the fun.
- **Ask the team** every few months what they enjoy and what they would drop.

> **Example scenario (not a real event):** A product team of 14 spread over three time zones starts every Monday call with "Guess the view". In week three, the photo of snowy mountains turns out to be from the developer everyone assumed lived in Florida. She moved to Colorado a year ago and never mentioned it. Now the team asks for a weekly snow report.

## Where does a live trivia game fit for remote and hybrid teams?

A live trivia game about the team works in one room, on a video call or both at once, like an offsite, a holiday party or a team day with half the team at home. For a hybrid team, it puts everyone in the same game.
